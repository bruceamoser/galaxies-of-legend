# Three-book editorial review

## Reference study

Reviewed the local Starfinder Core Rulebook, especially printed pages 16, 44, 292 and 324. Its character steps tell readers what to record, species pages give art and rules distinct jobs, and starship pages use consistent hierarchy to separate construction terms and action procedures. The Beginner Box Heroes’ Handbook and Game Master’s Guide show the value of teaching a procedure through a specific scene and placing the information needed at the moment of play. GoL uses original prose and art, its existing Oswald and Roboto typography, blue-teal rules and restrained red accents.

## Player’s Book

The opening relay job introduces the conversation at the table. Iona’s worked creation ledger follows the published sequence and shows separate DP pools, a duplicate grant, a class purchase and a gear receipt. Class entries now explain their role in a crew, with three compact signatures adjacent where they fit. The Medic has a dedicated briefing and a signature page so the complete treatment text can remain legible. The harm reference joins short continuations with the following section and carries the introduction into the first wound family.

## Sector Architect’s Book

The relay job carries from the first session through faction pressure, discoverable leads, opposition, hazards and payment. Encounter calibration follows practical encounter construction. Ruling and review instructions are together near the worksheets. Tables give procedure and consequence cells more width than short index columns. Redundant material was consolidated to clear short chapter continuations.

## Starship Manual

The opening reading guide starts with the crew’s task. Construction separates the frame and cost instructions from the Scout Cutter ledger, options and capacity reference. Operation explains rating, condition and wear through the same cutter. Jump preparation identifies the handoff between engineer, navigator and pilot. Reference sections run together, while records and their interactive fields retain independent pages.

Rules, numerical tables and card outcomes remain in their original source files except for explanatory copy in the edited chapters. The existing Starter Kit release remains the prior review snapshot. This pass produces local book PDFs for reading review.
