## Shopping rules

- Resolve equipment grants from cards and origins before buying.
- An item can be sold for half its listed cost where a buyer exists.
- Capacity is 10 + 3 × Brawn. Carrying more than Capacity imposes a Bane on Agility and Speed checks.
- A character may carry one item in each hand without penalty. Two-handed items require both hands.
