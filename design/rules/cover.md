# Cover and concealment

**Status:** Untested GoL playtest proposal.

Read the line from the attack's origin to the target. A barrier can give cover from one direction and leave the target exposed from another. If a barrier entirely blocks the attack, pick another line or an ability that can reach around it. Concealment such as darkness or steam uses the visibility rules and supplies no cover DR by itself.

| Cover on the attack line | Cover DR |
|---|---:|
| Open line or barrier that cannot impede the effect | 0 |
| Partial protection from a solid obstacle | 1 |
| Most of the target behind a substantial barrier | 2 |
| A protected firing slit or similar reinforced position | 3 |

Choose the one barrier that provides the best applicable cover. Announce whether it can impede the specific effect. An explosion that originates on the target's side of a barrier may bypass it. A structural vibration can reach someone touching the barrier even when a projectile cannot.

After applying matching personal resistance, subtract applicable cover DR once from the event's remaining total. A damaging event that reaches the target deals at least 1 total damage. Cover DR represents the obstacle and is separate from worn Armor DR, which remains Physical resistance. If a card bypasses cover DR, reduce the barrier's value by the amount stated, to a minimum of zero. Complete obstruction is a targeting limit, so an effect that merely ignores cover DR cannot pass through it.

Changing position can change which line is protected. Mark the barrier and the squares it protects. A character can use an ordinary move to reach cover, and only gains a separate defense Boon from an action or card that grants it.
