# Moser's law of conservation of Life energy

Healing magic transfers life energy; it does not create it freely. A Novice healing
spell costs its caster **1 HP**, an Adept spell **3 HP**, and a Master spell **7 HP**.
Pay the full cost before rolling. This cost bypasses all resistance and must leave
the caster above 0 HP. You cannot begin a healing spell by sacrificing your last HP.
The cost applies regardless of the tradition used to describe the spell.

Healing requires a free hand. Restore the outcome's printed fixed HP, reduce it by
the recipient's Life resistance, and restore at least 1 if HP is missing. Never
exceed maximum HP. Healing does not erase wounds or reverse Last Words. Potions have
their own fixed value and inventory procedure, without a spell outcome roll or caster cost.

> **Example — energy has a price.** A Novice healer at 5 HP pays 1 and falls to 4,
> then rolls the spell. If its outcome restores 4 HP to a companion with Life
> resistance 2, the companion regains 2. The caster cannot use the same spell at
> 1 HP, because paying first would leave them at 0.

A healing spell can attack a creature whose profile says healing harms it. Use the
spell's attack attribute, range and cost. Its HP result becomes Life damage; beneficial
recovery riders do not help that target. This follows the ordinary player-roll
procedure, with no extra opposed save.


## Applying the foundation in GOL

The inherited cost is **1 / 3 / 7 HP for Novice / Adept / Master rolled HP-healing abilities**, paid first and bypassing resistance, leaving the healer above 0. GOL's medical presentation does not waive that shared economy. Patch Wounds, Combat Medic and the Medic's HP-restoring signatures print their cost. Wound treatment that adds only recovery progress does not pay a healing HP cost. A medkit's ordinary consumable use remains separate from the healer's HP cost; neither is refunded on Weak.

Healing needs a free hand. Cap actual HP restored by missing HP, apply Life resistance and restore at least 1 where HP is missing. Dead characters and Last Words cannot be treated as ordinary HP targets. A Dying recipient must first be stabilized for ordinary rolled healing. A card that explicitly performs emergency stabilization, such as Field Triage or Last Light, can state that exception; it still cannot reverse Last Words.

A fixed-dose medical supply uses HOL's potion procedure: giver's Maneuver, free hand, another recipient's Reaction unless at 0 HP, fixed 6/10/16 HP, Life resistance, then an inventory-die check. No outcome roll and no healer HP payment. This is a science-fiction presentation of the inherited consumable, not an additional stronger healing system. No recipe, crafting permission or free dose is granted by the universal action.

Safe breath and Dying-turn Grit are their explicit full-HP recovery procedures, not rolled healing abilities. Neither restores Grit or removes a scar. Direct HP transfer, if explicitly granted by a future card, follows HOL's transfer rule: donor must stay above 0, recipient cannot exceed maximum, no Life resistance and no second conservation charge. No generic transfer action is granted here.
