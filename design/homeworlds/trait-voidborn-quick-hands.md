# Quick Hands

*You have restarted a failing console with one hand while holding a hatch closed with the other.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Agility checks to manipulate, reload, repair or sabotage equipment under pressure.

**Homeworld:** Voidborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-voidborn-quick-hands
