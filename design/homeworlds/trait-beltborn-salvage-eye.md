# Salvage Eye

*You can tell a dead relay from a valuable one at a glance.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Reason checks to spot valuable salvage, usable parts or hidden damage in wreckage and industrial spaces.

**Homeworld:** Beltborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-beltborn-salvage-eye
