# Tight Quarters

*You know how to fold yourself into spaces that were not designed for a person.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Agility checks to squeeze, climb or move quietly through cramped corridors and service ways.

**Homeworld:** Beltborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-beltborn-tight-quarters
