# Rock Sense

*Asteroids move in patterns that other people call luck.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Navigation checks in asteroid fields, debris belts or other crowded orbital terrain.

**Homeworld:** Beltborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-beltborn-rock-sense
