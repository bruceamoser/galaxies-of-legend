# Voidborn

*The ship was your country, the corridor your street, and the stars the weather.*

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. These are invitations to a familiar kind of
origin, not a required personality. Any homeworld may grow up in any culture.

**Starting values:** HP +1; base Speed 7.

- [Ship Rat](trait-voidborn-ship-rat.md) — Gain a Boon on Subterfuge checks aboard a ship or station to hide, eavesdrop, or reach a restricted compartment.
- [Low-Light Eyes](trait-voidborn-low-light-eyes.md) — You can see in dim light as though it were normal light.
- [Radiation Hardened](trait-voidborn-radiation-hardened.md) — Gain a Boon on Fortitude checks against radiation exposure, and reduce radiation exposure progress by 1 per scene.
- [Quick Hands](trait-voidborn-quick-hands.md) — Gain a Boon on Agility checks to manipulate, reload, repair or sabotage equipment under pressure.
- [Drift Legs](trait-voidborn-drift-legs.md) — Gain a Boon on Piloting checks to handle a ship or vehicle in microgravity or unusual drift.

**Starting coin contribution:** +1 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
