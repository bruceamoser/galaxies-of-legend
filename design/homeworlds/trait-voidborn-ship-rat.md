# Ship Rat

*You know the places a hull keeps for people who do not file reports.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Subterfuge checks aboard a ship or station to hide, eavesdrop, or reach a restricted compartment.

**Homeworld:** Voidborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-voidborn-ship-rat
