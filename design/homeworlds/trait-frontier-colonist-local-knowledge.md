# Local Knowledge

*You know which road floods, which neighbour keeps fuel, and which story is a warning.*

## Rules

Choose one settlement or region as your home. Gain a Boon on Navigation and social checks to find people, places or help there.

**Homeworld:** Frontier Colonist · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-frontier-colonist-local-knowledge
