# Drift Legs

*You move through a freefall deck without reaching for the rail.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Piloting checks to handle a ship or vehicle in microgravity or unusual drift.

**Homeworld:** Voidborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-voidborn-drift-legs
