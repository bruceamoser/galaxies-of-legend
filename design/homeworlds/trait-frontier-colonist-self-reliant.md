# Self-Reliant

*If it cannot be fixed with wire and patience, it can usually be fixed with more wire.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Engineering checks to improvise a repair without a proper toolkit.

**Homeworld:** Frontier Colonist · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-frontier-colonist-self-reliant
