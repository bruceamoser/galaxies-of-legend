# Media Literate

*You have watched enough official statements to hear the sentence being avoided.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Reason checks to notice deception or missing context in a public statement, broadcast or corporate notice.

**Homeworld:** Core World · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-core-world-media-literate
