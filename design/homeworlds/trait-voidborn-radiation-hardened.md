# Radiation Hardened

*Your family learned which parts of the ship to avoid and for how long.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Fortitude checks against radiation exposure, and reduce radiation exposure progress by 1 per scene.

**Homeworld:** Voidborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-voidborn-radiation-hardened
