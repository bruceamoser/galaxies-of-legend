# Stubborn

*You have been told to stop. You are still here.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Fortitude checks to resist exhaustion, deprivation or being moved against your will.

**Homeworld:** Frontier Colonist · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-frontier-colonist-stubborn
