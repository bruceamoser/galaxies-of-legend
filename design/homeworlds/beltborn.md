# Beltborn

*You learned to move through a refinery's ribs before you learned to walk a planet's weight.*

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. These are invitations to a familiar kind of
origin, not a required personality. Any homeworld may grow up in any culture.

**Starting values:** HP +2; base Speed 6.

- [Zero-G Native](trait-beltborn-zero-g-native.md) — You do not take the usual Bane for acting in zero gravity.
- [Vacuum Nerve](trait-beltborn-vacuum-nerve.md) — Gain a Boon on checks to resist fear or panic caused by vacuum, hull breach or decompression.
- [Salvage Eye](trait-beltborn-salvage-eye.md) — Gain a Boon on Reason checks to spot valuable salvage, usable parts or hidden damage in wreckage and industrial spaces.
- [Tight Quarters](trait-beltborn-tight-quarters.md) — Gain a Boon on Agility checks to squeeze, climb or move quietly through cramped corridors and service ways.
- [Rock Sense](trait-beltborn-rock-sense.md) — Gain a Boon on Navigation checks in asteroid fields, debris belts or other crowded orbital terrain.

**Starting coin contribution:** +0 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
