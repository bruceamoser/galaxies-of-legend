# Well Supplied

*Someone always made sure the right crate arrived before you knew you needed it.*

## Rules

At creation, gain one additional ordinary equipment bundle of Bulk 1 or less from the market. It must be carried normally.

**Homeworld:** Core World · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-core-world-well-supplied
