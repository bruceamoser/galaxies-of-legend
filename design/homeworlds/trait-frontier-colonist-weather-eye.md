# Weather Eye

*You can taste a storm in the air and see a bad season in the moss.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on Reason checks to predict weather, natural hazards or environmental danger in a region you have lived in.

**Homeworld:** Frontier Colonist · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-frontier-colonist-weather-eye
