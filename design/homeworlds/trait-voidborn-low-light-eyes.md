# Low-Light Eyes

*You can read a dark compartment by the light under a door.*

## Rules

You can see in dim light as though it were normal light. Total darkness still requires a light source.

**Homeworld:** Voidborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-voidborn-low-light-eyes
