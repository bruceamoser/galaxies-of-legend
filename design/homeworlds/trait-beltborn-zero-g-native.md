# Zero-G Native

*Microgravity is not a hazard to you; it is a familiar floor.*

## Rules

You do not take the usual Bane for acting in zero gravity. Gain a Boon on Agility checks to move, brace or manipulate equipment in microgravity.

**Homeworld:** Beltborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-beltborn-zero-g-native
