# Frontier Colonist

*You grew up at the end of the supply line, where a broken pump was a community emergency.*

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. These are invitations to a familiar kind of
origin, not a required personality. Any homeworld may grow up in any culture.

**Starting values:** HP +3; base Speed 6.

- [Hard Graft](trait-frontier-colonist-hard-graft.md) — Increase your maximum HP by 1.
- [Weather Eye](trait-frontier-colonist-weather-eye.md) — Gain a Boon on Reason checks to predict weather, natural hazards or environmental danger in a region you have lived in.
- [Self-Reliant](trait-frontier-colonist-self-reliant.md) — Gain a Boon on Engineering checks to improvise a repair without a proper toolkit.
- [Local Knowledge](trait-frontier-colonist-local-knowledge.md) — Choose one settlement or region as your home.
- [Stubborn](trait-frontier-colonist-stubborn.md) — Gain a Boon on Fortitude checks to resist exhaustion, deprivation or being moved against your will.

**Starting coin contribution:** +0 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
