# Core World

*You grew up where the lights never go out and every corridor has a camera with a budget.*

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. These are invitations to a familiar kind of
origin, not a required personality. Any homeworld may grow up in any culture.

**Starting values:** HP +2; base Speed 6.

- [Civic Access](trait-core-world-civic-access.md) — Once per scene, name a public record, permit or civic service you have used before.
- [Networked](trait-core-world-networked.md) — Once per scene, ask one contact for a useful fact about a person, place or organisation.
- [Media Literate](trait-core-world-media-literate.md) — Gain a Boon on Reason checks to notice deception or missing context in a public statement, broadcast or corporate notice.
- [Well Supplied](trait-core-world-well-supplied.md) — At creation, gain one additional ordinary equipment bundle of Bulk 1 or less from the market.
- [Cosmopolitan](trait-core-world-cosmopolitan.md) — Know two additional ordinary spoken languages.

**Starting coin contribution:** +1 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
