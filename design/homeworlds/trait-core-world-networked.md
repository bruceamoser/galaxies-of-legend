# Networked

*Your contacts are not powerful, but there are a great many of them.*

## Rules

Once per scene, ask one contact for a useful fact about a person, place or organisation. The answer is brief and may be incomplete. This is not a favour or a free service.

**Homeworld:** Core World · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-core-world-networked
