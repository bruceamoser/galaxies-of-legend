# Civic Access

*You know which office keeps which record, and which clerk is willing to be helpful.*

## Rules

Once per scene, name a public record, permit or civic service you have used before. The SA tells you where it is and what it will take to access it. This grants no forged authority.

**Homeworld:** Core World · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-core-world-civic-access
