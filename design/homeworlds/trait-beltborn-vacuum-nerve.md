# Vacuum Nerve

*You have heard the hull tick and kept working.*

## Rules

Gain a Boon on checks to resist fear or panic caused by vacuum, hull breach or decompression.

**Homeworld:** Beltborn · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-beltborn-vacuum-nerve
