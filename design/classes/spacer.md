# Spacer

*You keep the ship alive long enough for the plan to matter.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - expanded affinity profile, untested.

**In the crew.** Know what the ship can give you before you ask for more. Work with the other stations so the crew can act before a good approach closes.

## Rules

**Class foundations:** minimum 0 in Agility, Fortitude; HP contribution +3. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Engineering, Gunnery, Navigation, Piloting, Robotics.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Engineering, Piloting. Adjacent: Gunnery, Navigation, Robotics, Computing. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.
