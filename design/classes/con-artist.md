# Con Artist

*You make the room expect the person you are pretending to be.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - new class and signatures, untested.

## Rules

**Class foundations:** minimum 0 in Guile, Reason; HP contribution +1. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Influence, Subterfuge, Trade, Investigation, Computing.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Influence, Subterfuge. Adjacent: Trade, Investigation, Computing, Command. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

**Class role:** Prepared identities and leverage, distinct from the Envoy's open negotiation and leadership.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.
