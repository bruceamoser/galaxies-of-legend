# Soldier

*You make the hard choice in the space between one breath and the next.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - expanded affinity profile, untested.

## Rules

**Class foundations:** minimum 0 in Brawn, Fortitude; HP contribution +4. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Arms, Gunnery, Armor, Athletics, Command.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Arms, Gunnery, Armor. Adjacent: Athletics, Command, Medicine. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.
