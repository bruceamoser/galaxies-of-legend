# Operator

*You enter through the gap between physical and digital security.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - new class and signatures, untested.

## Rules

**Class foundations:** minimum 0 in Agility, Knowledge; HP contribution +2. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Subterfuge, Computing, Gunnery, Investigation, Robotics.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Subterfuge, Computing. Adjacent: Gunnery, Investigation, Athletics, Robotics. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

**Class role:** Covert entry and network intrusion, distinct from the Con Artist's social impersonation.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.
