# Outlaw

*You find work beyond the safe lanes and leave before the warrants arrive.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - new class and signatures, untested.

## Rules

**Class foundations:** minimum 0 in Agility, Guile; HP contribution +3. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Gunnery, Survival, Piloting, Subterfuge, Trade.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Gunnery, Survival. Adjacent: Piloting, Subterfuge, Trade, Athletics. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

**Class role:** Mobile gunfighter and frontier survivor, distinct from the Soldier's holding ground.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.
