# Reference and ship records

Use this reference after reading Chapters 2-4. If a summary is insufficient, return to the named full procedure rather than inventing a shorter substitute.

## Construction checks

1. Choose one frame. Copy its budget, module limit, die ceiling, Hull, Handling and Scale.
2. Buy exactly one engine, navigation suite, sensor system, life support and communications system, at least d4 each.
3. Buy optional modules individually. An FTL drive and capacitor bank are a pair; targeting needs a weapon; hangars require a medium frame or larger and at least d6.
4. Spend d4/d6/d8/d10/d12 costs of 1/2/3/4/5. An upgrade costs the difference. Do not exceed the budget, module limit or ceiling.
5. Copy installed dice to current dice. Derive capacity and active capability. Record separate mount IDs.
6. Assign real crew, fuel, parts and cargo. None is granted by leftover allocation.

## During a scene

| Event | Order |
|---|---|
| A station action | Declare action and primary equipment; apply quality and situation; resolve the Legend check; then roll named load dice. |
| A load die shows 1 | Step down one size; d4 becomes offline. The action already resolved. |
| Ship attack | One attack or player defence check; apply resistance, current shield DR and remaining Hull loss. |
| Shield use | Resolve the hit with pre-hit DR, then check the online generator, even for an absorbed hit. |
| Hull reduced | One wound d6: 1-2 Hull only; 3 sensors; 4 engines; 5 one mount; 6 capacitors or life support. Check the named module; only 1 degrades it. |
| Transfer | Move current steps from donor to receiver; never change installed allocation or create bigger hardware. |
| Disabled at 0 Hull | No engines, weapons or FTL; declare the three-step emergency and its consequence. |
| Personal injury to crew | Player's Book Chapter 7, not the ship wound d6. |

## Before a jump

Confirm effective drive range, capacitor capacity, route distance, fuel and destination. Both charge and navigation targets are three times the route distance. Ordinary charge costs one fuel per segment, at the bank's effective time; navigation uses ten-minute plotting intervals. Record the completing navigation band and complications.

The Pilot commits when both tracks and required systems are ready. Arrive, clear the tracks and check the drive. Emergency Jump can change the action timing stated on its card; it does not grant missing fuel, charge, a drive or a legal route.

## Record temporary changes honestly

Keep installed capability, underlying current wear and temporary adjustment separate. A boost does not erase wear. Repair does not invent a missing module. Full service restores the purchased rating. Write the donor, recipient, duration and any end-of-scene consequence for a transfer or temporary ability.
