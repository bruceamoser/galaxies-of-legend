# A ship the crew can understand

A patched cutter hangs under a dock light while the crew argues about what they can afford to keep. Better sensors would open the survey work. A larger hold would pay for repairs. An extra weapon means somebody must leave another station to fire it. These are the decisions this manual is built to expose.

The ship is a shared character with a purchased structure, installed systems and a changing condition. Its operators remain ordinary Legend characters. A system die measures equipment capability and endurance; the crew member still rolls 3d6 plus one attribute. The two rolls answer different questions.

## The three-book Starter Kit

| Volume | Owns |
|---|---|
| Player's Book | Character creation, advancement, learned abilities and the complete personal damage/recovery foundation. |
| Starship Manual | Ship construction, modules, station actions, ship combat, power transfer, repairs, FTL and ship records. |
| Sector Architect's Book | Adjudication, preparation, places, factions, encounters, hazards, threat seeds and campaign tools. |

A character's learned ship ability stays in the Player's Book, Chapters 4 and 8. Its explicit text takes precedence over a basic station action. The personal wound system stays in Player's Book Chapter 7; ship Hull is not a character HP pool with Grit and human injuries.

## Read this manual in order

Chapter 2 builds a ship by spending its size-based Ship Points (SP), then shows the completed Scout Cutter sheet. Chapter 3 explains what changes during use and how the crew operates it. Chapter 4 runs the two-track jump procedure. Chapter 5 shows three checked builds. Chapter 6 supplies a quick reference, the character-ability reading guide and the ship records.

Bring the Player's Book, one ship record, the crew's character sheets, three d6 for action checks and the system dice needed by the selected modules. Record actual fuel, parts, cargo and crew before play. The frame's Ship Point (SP) total does not buy those supplies or hire operators.

## Status of this edition

All construction budgets, system capacities, equipment grades, ship damage, repair quantities and FTL timing in this manual are **playtest proposals that have not been tested at a table**. The examples pass allocation checks; those checks do not certify balance. Ship prices, building labour, oversized weapons, boarding detail and ship advancement remain open design work.

Ancestry material is still being supplied by Bruce. This book grants no alien biology or character traits. It is the starship volume of the first Starter Kit review package, not an approval to begin a mission or a table playtest.
