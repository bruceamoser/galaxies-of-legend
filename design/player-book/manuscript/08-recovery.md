# Harm and Recovery

## The four tracks

| Track | What it means |
|---|---|
| **HP** | Immediate damage. Recovers after a safe rest in a secured place. |
| **Grit** | Composure, luck and staying power. Spend it; it returns slowly. |
| **Wounds** | Lasting injury. A wound leaves a scar even after treatment. |
| **Death marks** | The road toward death. Three marks and the character is gone. |

HP does not increase automatically with level. Record current and maximum HP separately.

## Reaching 0 HP

When damage reduces a character to 0 HP, they fall **Prone** and are **Dying**. At the start of each of their turns while Dying, they make a **death roll**: 3d6 + Fortitude against **10**.

- **Success:** remain at 0 HP and stable. No new death mark.
- **Failure:** gain 1 death mark. At 3 death marks, the character dies.
- **Strong:** remain stable and may speak or take one slow action.

A successful **Medicine** check to stabilise ends the Dying condition. Any healing that restores HP also ends it. A character who returns from 0 HP gains a **scar** and a lasting consequence chosen with the SA.

## Grit

A character starts with **2 Grit**. Spend 1 Grit to add a Boon to one check before rolling, or to reduce incoming damage by 2 (to a minimum of 1). Grit returns after a **safe rest** in a secured place. It does not return on a short pause in a danger zone.

## Wounds

When a character takes a **wound**, roll 1d6 on the table below. The wound's penalty lasts until treated; the scar remains.

| d6 | Wound | Effect |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Flesh wound** | No penalty; the scar is visible. |
| 2 | **Cracked rib** | Bane on Brawn checks until treated. |
| 3 | **Concussion** | Bane on Reason checks until treated. |
| 4 | **Deep cut** | Bleeding 1 until treated. |
| 5 | **Broken limb** | Slowed 1 and a Bane on Agility checks until treated. |
| 6 | **Internal injury** | Bleeding 2; the character is Dying until stabilised. |

Treatment is a **Medicine** check taking ten minutes in a safe place. The SA sets the difficulty (usually 10). On a success, remove the wound's penalty. The scar remains and counts toward the character's story, not their stats.

## Rest and recovery

- **Safe rest:** recover all HP and all Grit. Wounds remain.
- **Short pause:** no HP or Grit recovery; bind a wound, reload, or catch your breath.
- **Medical care:** a Medicine check can treat one wound per patient per day.
- **Death marks:** do not disappear with rest. Only an explicit rule or a major story event can remove one.

## Radiation and exposure

Radiation is both immediate damage and a separate exposure track. At the end of a scene in a contaminated area, gain **1 exposure**. At 3 exposure, gain a Bane on all Fortitude checks until treated. At 6 exposure, the character takes a wound. Treatment requires a medkit and a safe place.
