# Classes

When a salvage job goes wrong, someone has to decide what the crew can save. The Spacer can help the cutter hold position while the others work. The Technician can look for a way to get the crane moving. The Envoy can ask the dockmaster for an extra hour and offer something that makes the delay worthwhile. Which of them takes the lead depends on what the crew tries first.

Choose a class for the decisions you want to make often. Read its starting disciplines to see what you can learn early, then read its signatures to see how that role develops. If you want to play the person who keeps a crew safe, the Soldier and Medic offer different ways to do it. One can cover a dangerous approach. The other can help someone survive what happens when the approach goes wrong.

Each class grants two starting discipline choices. Meet both attribute minimums before choosing your class. Choose one class for your character. At levels 1, 3 and 7 you gain one free signature from your class at that milestone. Shared abilities remain open to anyone who meets their requirements.

A ship station describes where you are working during a scene. The pilot may need to leave the controls to help a wounded crewmate, and another explorer can take the seat. Look in the Starship Manual for the station's procedures and here for the abilities that your character brings to the job.
