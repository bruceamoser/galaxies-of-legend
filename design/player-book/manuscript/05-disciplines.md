# Disciplines

A discipline is a field of training. Its ranks are **prerequisites**, not passive bonuses. A rank qualifies you to buy the abilities that require it. It does not add a number to every roll, grant equipment or let you use every card in the field.

## Ranks and prices

Disciplines have ranks **1, 2 and 3**. Buy them in order: rank 2 requires rank 1, and rank 3 requires rank 2. A class sets the price of each rank.

| Price band | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Home** | 1 DP | 2 DP | 4 DP |
| **Adjacent** | 2 DP | 4 DP | 8 DP |
| **Foreign** | 3 DP | 6 DP | 12 DP |
| **Opposed** | 4 DP | 8 DP | 16 DP |

Every rank is priced separately. Cheap ranks are a class advantage because they let you reach abilities earlier. A class that gets cheap ranks is not also owed stronger signatures; compare the whole class.

## The nine fields

- **Arms** — close combat and the grammar of a fight.
- **Gunnery** — ranged weapons, suppression and ship weapons.
- **Piloting** — starships, vehicles and evasive handling.
- **Navigation** — astrogation, survey and FTL plotting.
- **Engineering** — power, repairs, fabrication and ship systems.
- **Science** — analysis, xenobiology, physics and computing.
- **Medicine** — trauma care, stabilisation and surgery.
- **Subterfuge** — stealth, security, deception and the black market.
- **Influence** — command, negotiation, morale and bureaucracy.

## Choose a direction

You do not need to own a discipline to attempt an ordinary action in that field. The discipline matters when an ability prints a rank requirement. A character with no Gunnery rank can still fire a weapon; they cannot buy the cards that make them a extraordinary shot.

When you build a character, ask three questions:

1. What do I want to be reliably good at in a crisis?
2. Which discipline unlocks the abilities I actually want to use?
3. What is my class already making cheap, and what is it making expensive?

A narrow character with three ranks in one field and one rank in two others is as valid as a broad character with many rank-1 fields. The catalog has no required chain and no ability that requires an earlier ability unless it says so.
