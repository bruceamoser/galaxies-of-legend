# Ancestry, Culture and Background

At Waycross, a Silev checks the cutter’s service record while an Ambril helps fit a heat shield and a Pellori waits to inspect the cargo. All three grew up around the same docks. They learned the same local customs and took different jobs when the freight lines opened.

Build your explorer from those layers. Ancestry gives you a body and a choice of two traits. Culture is the community that taught you how to get along. Background is the experience that gave you a practical skill and a reason to leave. Take one complete culture pair and one complete background pair. Chapter 2 explains what to do if their grants overlap.

When you read the ancestries, think of a scene you would like to play. A Nacreth might steady a line in rushing water. A Weldkin might keep a clamp steady while a friend works. The same person might also be the best negotiator on the crew, depending on what they learned and where they learned it. Appendix A gives each lineage a point of departure in the galaxy.
