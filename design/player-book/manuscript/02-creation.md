# Create Your Explorer

You build a character in the same order every time. Each choice has a separate job: attributes define what you can do, your ancestry gives you a body and a history, culture and background give you a paired discipline and ability, and your class gives you training, starting ranks and a signature.

## 1. Assign attributes

Legend uses six attributes. Assign a value from **−2 to +2** to each. The values must total **+3**.

| Attribute | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Brawn** | Force, endurance, close combat; contributes to Capacity |
| **Fortitude** | Bodily resistance, death rolls; contributes to HP |
| **Agility** | Coordination, movement, ranged attacks, piloting |
| **Guile** | Deception, improvisation, social leverage |
| **Knowledge** | Training, technical work, analysis |
| **Reason** | Perception, judgment, awareness |

The standard array is **+2, +1, +1, +0, +0, −1**. You may rearrange it, or use another array that totals +3 and stays within the range. Attributes are fixed after creation unless a rule says otherwise.

## 2. Choose an ancestry

Ancestry is inherited physiology; culture is upbringing. The final ancestry package will provide HP, base Speed, a coin contribution and five comparable traits, choose two. Alien ancestry material is coming from Bruce.

This review contains **placeholders**, not finished ancestries. For arithmetic examples only, Human uses HP +2, base Speed 6 and +0 ancestry coins, with no granted traits. Alien A, B and C have no statistics, biology or benefits. Do not treat these slots as complete character options. The old homeworld packages are legacy environmental drafts and are excluded from creation.

## 3. Choose one culture and one background

Each culture and each background is a **paired grant**: one rank-1 discipline and one ability that belongs with it. Take the complete pair. You cannot take the discipline from one option and the ability from another.

- **Culture** is the community that shaped you.
- **Background** is a formative job, training or life experience.

If a grant duplicates something you already have, gain **1 DP** in the creation-stage pool. A duplicate does not raise a discipline rank or give you a different ability.

## 4. Spend the level-0 DP pool

Before choosing a class, you have a pool of:

> **10 + Knowledge + Reason + Guile + duplicate-grant DP**

Spend it on **Novice abilities only**. You cannot buy discipline ranks at this stage. Unspent DP expires when you close the ledger.

## 5. Choose a class

A class gives you:

- two attribute minimums (usually 0);
- an HP contribution;
- a starting coin contribution;
- a list of disciplines from which you choose **two distinct rank-1** disciplines;
- a price profile for discipline ranks;
- one signature ability at each of levels 1, 3 and 7.

You must meet the class's attribute minimums. You may choose only one class; there is no multiclassing. Another class's signatures remain exclusive.

At level 1, choose one level-1 signature. You choose the level-3 signature when you reach level 3, and the level-7 signature at level 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP if a rule allows them.

## 6. Spend the level-1 DP pool

You have a second, separate pool of:

> **10 + Knowledge + Reason + Guile + duplicate-grant DP**

Spend it on discipline ranks at your class's prices and on eligible abilities. Points cannot move between the level-0 and level-1 pools. Unspent DP expires when you close the ledger.

## 7. Resolve equipment and derived values

Resolve all granted equipment first, then shop with your starting coins.

| Value | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Maximum HP** | 12 + ancestry HP + class HP + Fortitude + explicit benefits |
| **Capacity** | 10 + 3 × Brawn |
| **Speed** | ancestry base Speed + Agility − encumbrance, minimum 0 |
| **Starting Grit** | 2 + explicit increases |
| **Starting coins** | 40 + ancestry + culture + background + class contributions |

Record current and maximum HP separately. HP does not increase automatically with level.

## 8. Check the character

- Every prerequisite is owned before you buy the ability.
- Every duplicate grant has been converted to 1 DP, not a free rank.
- You have not spent the same DP twice.
- Your carried Bulk does not exceed Capacity without the stated penalty.
- You know which hand holds which item.
- Your wounds and death marks start at zero.

## Advancement

The reference progression runs to **level 10**.

- At level 2 and later, gain **8 + new level** DP. These points may be saved.
- **Adept** abilities become available at level 3.
- **Master** abilities become available at level 7.
- Class signatures are chosen at levels 1, 3 and 7.
- Gain **+1 Grit** at levels 3 and 7; this does not refill earlier expenditure. Ordinary rest never refreshes Grit.
- HP does not rise automatically with level.

A character who reaches level 10 has had many opportunities to broaden their training. The game does not assume every character buys every ability in their disciplines.
