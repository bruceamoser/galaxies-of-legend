# Using the Starship Manual

Before your crew leaves port, decide who will fly the ship, who will watch the sensors and who can reach the systems most likely to fail. Those are stations. An explorer can change jobs when the crew needs them somewhere else. Record the installed systems and current dice on the ship sheet before you start.

## Play at the right scale

Use Chapter 7 of this book when someone aboard takes a personal injury. Use the Starship Manual when the ship takes Hull damage or a system wears down. A ship hit and a personal injury can come from the same event. Resolve each at its own scale.

When you work a station, roll 3d6 plus one attribute and apply the system’s equipment quality as Boons or Banes. A separate load roll determines whether an active system wears down. Keep the installed rating and the current die on the record so the next operator knows what to expect.
