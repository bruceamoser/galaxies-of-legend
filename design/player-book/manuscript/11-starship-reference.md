# Using the Starship Manual

The crew's ship has its own book: the **Galaxies of Legend Starship Manual**. That book owns ship construction, installed and current system dice, crew stations, ship combat, power transfer, damage control, FTL and printable ship sheets. Keep it beside the Player's Book whenever the ship becomes the focus of a scene.

## Which book to use

| Question | Book and section |
|---|---|
| How do I make and advance a character? | Player's Book, Chapters 2-5. |
| Which character ability do I use aboard ship? | Player's Book, Chapters 4 and 8. Its action, attribute, prerequisites and outcomes remain authoritative. |
| How do we choose a frame and allocate module dice? | Starship Manual, Chapter 2: Build Your Ship. |
| How do system grades, stations, shields and ship damage work? | Starship Manual, Chapter 3: Operate Your Ship. |
| How do we charge, plot and commit an FTL jump? | Starship Manual, Chapter 4: FTL Preparation and Travel. |
| Where are the budgeted ship examples? | Starship Manual, Chapter 5: Example Builds. |
| Where are the ship records and construction checks? | Starship Manual, Chapter 6: Reference and Ship Records. |
| How does the SA prepare opposition, places and consequences? | Sector Architect's Book, Chapters 2-8. |

## Characters aboard a ship

Pilot, Gunner, Engineer, Sensor Operator, Navigator and Commander are station jobs, not separate character classes. The crew still uses the Legend attributes, result bands, Boons and Banes. A character's printed ability is not replaced by a better improvisation simply because the character is at a console.

Ship-related class signatures and shared abilities remain in this book because they are learned character capabilities. Their equipment and ship context are explained in the Starship Manual. A discipline rank does not add a skill bonus, and a system die is not added to an attribute total.

## Personal danger remains here

The full personal damage, Grit, Dying, Last Words, condition, wound and healing rules remain in **Chapter 7 of this book**, including all 280 wound results. They apply to crew members injured in a corridor, breach, boarding action or other personal hazard. A ship's Hull and module wear use the separate ship procedure; a ship does not spend personal Grit or roll a crew wound table for structural damage.

## Before using the ship proposal

The Starship Manual is an untested construction and operating proposal. It states its die budgets, capability tables and open economic questions explicitly. Decide which optional systems exist on the vessel before a scene; a character's background does not grant an unallocated cargo bay, med bay, weapon or FTL drive.

The three-book Starter Kit is a coordinated design review. It does not finish the ancestry placeholders or establish that the proposed ship and encounter numbers are ready for table playtesting.
