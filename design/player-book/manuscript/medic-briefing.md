# Medic briefing

## Decide what can wait

The crew has pulled two people out of the relay. One is Dying. The other can talk and move, but has a wound that will need attention once there is somewhere safe to work. Start by asking what will happen before your next turn. Stabilize can buy time for the first casualty. Triage can help when you need to divide your attention. The wound itself may call for a longer treatment once the immediate danger passes.

Before using a medical card, check its target, time, medkit use and HP cost. The Medic pays the printed HP cost and must stay above zero. Keep an eye on the supplies as well as the people. A safe breath can restore HP. A scar stays on the sheet, and treatment progress follows the wound's own Recovery Value.

## Read the signatures with Chapter 7 open

Triage covers urgent care when several people are hurt. Field Surgery gives you an hour-long treatment option for particular wounds. Last Light is a Reaction at the moment someone falls, after their new wound is determined. Read the exact card before deciding which one you can use.
