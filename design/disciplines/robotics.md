# Robotics

*You stop the machine from injuring the person trying to repair it.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - expanded field, untested.

## Rules

Robots, drones, autonomy, control safeguards and remote platforms. Robotics handles machine behaviour; Engineering handles broken components.

Ranks 1, 2 and 3 are purchased in order. Ranks unlock abilities; they add no passive bonus. Home 1/2/4 DP; Adjacent 2/4/8; Foreign 3/6/12; Opposed 4/8/16. No discipline purchases at level 0.

## Coverage direction

Novice: reliable basic task and one support option. Adept: a pressured multi-step task and a meaningful reaction or alternative approach. Master: a defining high-stakes application with explicit limits. This draft adds one starter ability; the remaining coverage is scheduled in waves.
