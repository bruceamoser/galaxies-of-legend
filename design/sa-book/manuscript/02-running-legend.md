# Running Legend at the table

Begin with intent and approach. What does the character want, what do they actually do and what is at stake? Obvious actions work without dice; impossible actions do not become possible because a player can roll well. When uncertainty has a consequence, choose the attribute or use the one printed on the declared ability.

## One roll and fixed bands

Roll 3d6 plus one of Brawn, Fortitude, Agility, Guile, Knowledge or Reason. Weak is 8 or less, Standard 9-14 and Strong 15 or more. A discipline rank is a prerequisite, not a skill modifier. Difficulty changes the Boons and Banes, never the result thresholds. Do not add a second target number such as 10 or 15 on top of the bands.

Boons and Banes cancel one for one. Add at most three net extra dice and keep the highest or lowest three. Three retained natural 1s mean Weak plus a complication; three retained 6s mean Strong plus a benefit. A complication does not delete progress or replace a printed Weak outcome with nothing.

State the consequence before commitment. One Bane can represent a meaningful obstacle, two a severe obstacle and three an exceptional but possible attempt. Those are adjudication guides, not a promise that every hard action has three Banes. Do not charge the same slippery surface twice as both terrain and a second invented numerical penalty.

## Respect the chosen card

The player declares their ability before rolling. Its action, attribute, range, targets and equipment determine what can happen. Read the one outcome achieved; do not add Weak damage to Standard damage. A Strong result does not grant an extra action or a system wound unless printed.

Long actions take their full time. A treatment card marked Main Action and one hour does not resolve in one combat turn. An equipment requirement grants no item; a supply already expended is not restored because another character learned the same ability.

## Information and repeated attempts

Give obvious information freely. Inspecting the object that holds an essential clue should reveal the clue. A roll can determine time, extra context or who notices. Prepare several routes to important conclusions, rather than a single successful search that the whole session depends on.

For an uncertain consequential search use Reason. A concealed player character rolls Agility when detection matters, with no extra opposed observer roll. Hidden is a universal state and needs plausible concealment. Invisible does not erase footprints, sound or other evidence of location. Attacking or open revelation ends Hidden; it does not automatically end Invisible.

If an identical attempt can be repeated freely without changing time, risk or resources, summarize the work. Where another attempt has a real cost, say what changes. A new approach can change the situation instead of merely buying another roll against an invisible threshold.

## Progress and teamwork

An ordinary cumulative task has a stated target and a stated time per attempt. Weak/Standard/Strong adds 1/2/3 progress. The target is a project quantity, not a different success threshold. Do not raise it whenever another helper joins.

For a group task outside combat, declare whether it is binary, requiring its target in one attempt, or cumulative, keeping progress. Each meaningful participant contributes 1/2/3. A participant contributes a roll or a printed support ability to the attempt, not both. Group participation does not compress wound treatment, crafting or derivation; use those activities' own procedures. Do not create a generic free Aid action or a second contribution merely from a character's narrative expertise.

## Combat timing and rolls

At combat start roll one unmodified 3d6: 12 or more gives the players the first turn; otherwise the opposition starts. Alternate sides, each choosing its next character. The same side starts each round. Every actor has one Main Action, one Maneuver, movement and one Reaction. Main can convert down to Maneuver, Maneuver down to movement; never upward.

A player attack uses one attacker roll and the printed outcome. An NPC attack uses the affected player's defence roll: Weak defence takes Strong attack effects, Standard takes Standard and Strong takes Weak. Defence itself spends no Reaction. For NPC against NPC, roll the defender's printed defence attribute and read the attack's inverted outcomes. For a consequential NPC healing or technical task, roll the acting NPC's printed attribute and outcomes. Handling dice does not give the player ownership of the NPC.

For a player area attack, roll once using the worst applicable net Boon/Bane situation among affected targets; apply individual resistances. An NPC area attack allows each affected player their own defence roll. A solo enemy has one turn and one Reaction, not hidden extra actions.

## Space, cover and capture

Use the Player's Book movement rules: normal squares cost 1, difficult cost 2, standing costs 2 movement and occupied squares can be crossed only when the occupant is Prone, at cost 2. No attack can pass through a completely blocked path. Partial cover gives the attack one Bane, heavy cover three; do not also add an armour value for that same cover.

Stability starts at 0 unless explicitly granted, highest source to a maximum of 3. Deduct it once per newly struck creature during forced movement, at the timing printed in the full rules. Wound-table pushes use these same collision rules; they do not invent an airborne damage system.

Attacks are lethal unless an explicit ability permits another result. Grappling needs the appropriate ability; it is not automatically granted by Basic Melee. An opponent can surrender or be restrained once the situation permits it. Do not turn every conversation into an attack roll or every defeated adversary into a player-character Dying sequence.

## Keep the full survival rules

Use Player's Book Chapter 7 for all personal injury. Safe breath restores HP, not Grit; scars remain. Each entry into Dying causes the full scar-weighted wound roll. At Dying-turn start choose Grit or the death roll before seeing dice. Universal Stabilize is an adjacent no-roll Maneuver. Three marks or wound 666 starts Last Words, ending in death at the end of the second following turn.

The six wound treatment capabilities take an hour and share one attempt per wound per day across all healers, adding 1/2/3 toward RV. A med bay supports a capability; it does not replace one. None of these procedures is replaced by a single difficulty-10 Medicine roll. Keep the 280-result reference available when a character falls.
