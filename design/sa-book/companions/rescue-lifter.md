## Drag to Cover

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing or incapacitated person weighing no more than an ordinary explorer and a clear adjacent destination.

Move one square into the destination and draw the person into the vacated square if both fit. Follow the forced movement and terrain rules.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
