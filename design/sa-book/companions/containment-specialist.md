## Set the Barrier

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One reachable clear doorway and an available portable barrier sized for it.

Place and secure the barrier as far as its fittings allow. Its physical rating determines what it stops. It cannot seal a pressure breach unless it is rated for it.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
