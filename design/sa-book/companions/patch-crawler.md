# Patch Crawler

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested personal companion.

**Family:** Drone · **Minimum controller level:** 3 · **Purchase price:** 185 coins

**Access:** Paired Drone Controller; working chassis and clear command signal.

**HP:** 8 · **Speed:** Ground 4; climbs ferrous surfaces at Speed 4 · **Footprint:** one square · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 1.

**Defence:** Agility 0.

**Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge +1; Reason 0.

**Senses and reporting:** Ordinary camera and microphone within working environment. Reports only during a paid order.

## Hold the Patch

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One reachable seam and a patch already supplied and fitted to it.

Hold the patch in place until the start of the controller’s next turn or until the drone moves. This provides a spare hand for a repair and does not complete the repair.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
