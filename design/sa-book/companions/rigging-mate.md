## Set the Second Line

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One sound anchor within reach and one ready line.

Clip the second line to the anchor and tell the controller its endpoint. This does not test a hidden structural fault.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
