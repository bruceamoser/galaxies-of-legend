## Map the Authorized Route

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible service and its available routing logs.

List the documented hops between the host and that service, flagging missing records. This does not discover a hidden service or connect to new nodes.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
