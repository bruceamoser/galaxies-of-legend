## Repeat the Terms

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing speaker within conversation range using a language agreed in the contract.

Interpret one short statement into the agreed shared language. Ask for clarification when the speaker uses an unknown term.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
