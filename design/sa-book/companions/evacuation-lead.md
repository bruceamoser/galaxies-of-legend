# Evacuation Lead

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested personal companion.

**Family:** Hire · **Minimum controller level:** 7 · **Sample fee for one agreed shift:** 120 coins

**Access:** Consenting NPC and a contract covering the listed work. Ordinary tools are supplied only as agreed.

**HP:** 11 · **Speed:** Ground 5 · **Footprint:** one square · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 0.

**Defence:** Agility 0.

**Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason +1.

**Senses and reporting:** Ordinary sight and hearing; communicates in the languages agreed at hiring.

## Direct One Transfer

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing adjacent patient and one willing carrier within speaking distance.

Describe where to grip and where to set the patient down. The carrier gains a Boon on their next check this round to move that patient. This restores no HP.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
