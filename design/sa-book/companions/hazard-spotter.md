**Defence:** Agility 0.

**Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason +1.

## Point Out the Boundary

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible hazard and one willing ally within speaking distance.

Identify the visible edge. Give the ally a Boon on their next check this round to avoid entering it. This grants no protection inside it.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
