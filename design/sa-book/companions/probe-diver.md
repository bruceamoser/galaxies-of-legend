## Read the Waterline

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One reachable submerged seal or joint in clear water.

Report the visible condition and whether bubbles or debris show a leak. It does not locate a leak beyond its view.

**Limits:** Use one paid command package under Drones, Sprites and Companions in Player’s Book Chapter 9. No independent activity or Reaction.
