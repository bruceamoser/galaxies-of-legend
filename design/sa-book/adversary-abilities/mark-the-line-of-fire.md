# Mark the Line of Fire

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / trigger:** One visible approach already inside the actor’s range and firing arc.

Mark it with a visible beam or an audible warning. Anyone who perceives the warning can identify the approach; this grants no attack.
