# Vent the Steam

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / trigger:** One adjacent open square and an available steam outlet or hot coolant discharge.

Vent a visible cloud in the square until the start of the actor’s next turn. Sight through the cloud is blocked. The cloud causes no damage.

**ID:** adversary-vent-the-steam
