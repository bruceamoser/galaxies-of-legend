# Interpose Plate

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / trigger:** Before a visible melee attack against an adjacent ally is rolled; ready shield or rigid plate.

If the barrier can be placed between the ally and the attacker, apply a Bane when the attacker rolls, or a Boon when the ally rolls defense. The ally remains the target.

**ID:** adversary-interpose-plate
