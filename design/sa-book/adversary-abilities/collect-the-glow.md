# Collect the Glow

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / trigger:** One bright visible lamp or direct light source within 3 squares.

Face the light. If it remains lit, the next attack of this actor this turn gains a Boon on the acting roll, or imposes one Bane on the target’s defense if the target rolls instead.

**ID:** adversary-collect-the-glow
