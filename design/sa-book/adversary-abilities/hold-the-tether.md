# Hold the Tether

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / trigger:** When an unattended item attached to a tether starts drifting out of reach.

Catch the tether with a free hand if it can carry the load. This does not stop the actor from being pulled if it lacks an anchor.

**ID:** adversary-hold-the-tether
