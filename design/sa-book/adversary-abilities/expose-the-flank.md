# Expose the Flank

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / trigger:** One visible foe and one willing ally who can hear the cue.

Point out an unprotected route. The ally gets a Boon on their next check this round to reach cover along that route.

**ID:** adversary-expose-the-flank
