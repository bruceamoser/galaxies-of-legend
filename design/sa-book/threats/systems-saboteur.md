# Systems Saboteur

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale threat seed; untested and no calibrated CV.

**Family:** Crews

**Role:** A technician interfering with a specific accessible device.

**HP:** 10 · **Speed:** 6 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 0

**Defence:** Agility +1. **Other attributes:** Knowledge +1; Agility +1; Guile 0. Unlisted attributes are 0; no separate skill bonus.

## Concealed pistol

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** one visible target within 6 squares.

- **Weak:** 1 Kinetic damage.
- **Standard:** 2 Kinetic damage.
- **Strong:** 3 Kinetic damage.

## Interfere

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target:** one reachable, powered, accessible nonliving device.

- **Weak:** 1 progress; leave a visible trace.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

## Equipment and limits

Toolkit and pistol. Interfere is a cumulative 3-progress task to stop one ordinary control function; name the device and countermeasure before the first roll. It cannot destroy a ship or cancel a learned ability.

## Behaviour

Needs physical or already established network access. Repair or restored control reverses the stopped function; the profile grants no remote omnipotence. This interference fixture is untested.
