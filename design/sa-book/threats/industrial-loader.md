# Industrial Loader

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale threat seed; untested and no calibrated CV.

**Family:** Security and synthetics

**Role:** A heavy work machine operating in a dangerous or contested space.

**HP:** 20 · **Speed:** 4 squares · **Stability:** 2 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility -1. **Other attributes:** Brawn +2; Agility -1; Reason 0. Unlisted attributes are 0; no separate skill bonus.

## Manipulator impact

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** one adjacent target.

- **Weak:** 3 Kinetic damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Kinetic damage.
- **Strong:** 6 Kinetic damage and push 1 square.

## Equipment and limits

Heavy manipulator; no inherent ranged attack. Nonliving: compatible device repair instead of ordinary HP healing. Its printed Stability 2 is an explicit fixture, not calculated from mass.

## Behaviour

Moves along an open route to its work site. The Strong push uses ordinary Stability, terrain and collision rules. An operator-controlled loader uses that operator's Main Action rather than receiving a second independent turn.
