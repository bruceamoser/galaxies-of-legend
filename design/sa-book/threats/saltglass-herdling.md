**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Role:** Defends mineral feeding sites on a tidal flat.

**HP:** 9 · **Speed:** 6 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility +0. **Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude 0; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

## Crystal Horn

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible target within 1 square and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Physical damage.

## Yield the Flat

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** The herdling when a visible retreat opens toward its next tide channel.

Move one square toward the channel if the route is clear. This does not carry it through occupied space.

It scrapes bright salt fans into the ground before charging. Drive intruders off the feeding site and stop at the next tide channel.
