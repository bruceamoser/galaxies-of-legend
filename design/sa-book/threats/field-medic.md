# Field Medic

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale threat seed; untested and no calibrated CV.

**Family:** Crews

**Role:** A person trying to keep a working group alive.

**HP:** 10 · **Speed:** 6 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 0

**Defence:** Agility +0. **Other attributes:** Knowledge +1; Reason 0; Agility 0. Unlisted attributes are 0; no separate skill bonus.

## Emergency HP care

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target:** one adjacent willing, living, stable recipient.

- **Weak:** restore 2 HP.
- **Standard:** restore 4 HP.
- **Strong:** restore 6 HP and reduce temporary Bleeding by 1.

## Equipment and limits

Medkit with five uses. Pay 1 HP before the healing roll, bypassing resistance and leaving the medic above 0; free hand; consume one use. Apply Life resistance, minimum 1 if HP is missing. Can use universal Stabilize as its Maneuver before healing.

## Behaviour

Has no attack printed here and can seek cover, move or retreat. Care restores no wounds, Grit or life after Last Words. The profile does not receive unlimited healing because an SA controls it.
