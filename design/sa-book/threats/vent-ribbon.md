# Vent Ribbon

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Station fauna

**Role:** Waits in overhead grilles for airborne prey.

**HP:** 7 · **Speed:** 6 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 0

**Defence:** Agility +1. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility +1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Climbs intact ducts and grilles at its listed Speed.

## Ribbon Lash

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** One visible target within 2 squares and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 4 Physical damage. Pull the target 1 square toward the ribbon along clear ground.

## Fold into the Duct

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** The ribbon next to its open grille.

Climb one square into the grille if it is unobstructed. Use the resulting position to determine sight and cover.

## Equipment and limits

Uses the ordinary NPC defeat procedure. No additional immunities or recovery abilities. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

Dust hangs from its folded body just before it drops. Pull back into its grille when approached from two directions.
