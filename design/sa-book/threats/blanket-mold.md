# Blanket Mold

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** The Slop

**Role:** A thick felted mat hangs from overhead braces. The outline of an old meal shows in its folds.

**HP:** 15 · **Speed:** 4 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 0

**Defence:** Fortitude 0. **Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude 0; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Climbs sound walls and ceilings at Speed 4.

## Drop and Wrap

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible adjacent creature under a reachable overhead support.

- **Weak:** 1 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 4 Physical damage. On a Strong attack, the target gains maintained Grappled 1 if the Mold is holding nobody. The Mold cannot hold a second creature.

## Hoist the Meal

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** One creature it is holding, with a clear line to an overhead anchor.

Pull the target 2 squares toward the anchor under ordinary Stability. The Mold stays within reach by moving along its support by the same distance if the path is legal; otherwise it cannot hoist.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Use the Overhead Route](../adversary-abilities/use-the-overhead-route.md)

## Equipment and limits

Living colony. Ordinary NPC defeat and healing apply. No unlisted immunities or senses. When the target escapes, when the Mold is defeated, or when an adjacent explorer cuts the accessible anchoring strands with a suitable cutting tool as a Main Action, the grapple ends. If the target is unsupported, it drops into the nearest clear square directly below. This profile’s hoist deals no extra damage on release. If the scene has a separate drop hazard, announce its consequence before the hoist.

## Behaviour

The ceiling mat sags before dropping. Stay clear of the anchor or keep a rescuer near the cutting point.
