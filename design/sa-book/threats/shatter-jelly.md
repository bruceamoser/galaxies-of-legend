# Shatter Jelly

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** The Slop

**Role:** Glassy plates overlap a clear body. It moves by tipping its weight from plate to plate.

**HP:** 17 · **Speed:** 4 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Fortitude 0. **Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude 0; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Ground movement.

## Shard Sweep

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Physical damage. On a Strong attack, the target gains Bleeding 1.

## Shear a Fragment

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** Self, after taking Physical damage from a visible attack and surviving with at least 4 HP.

Once per encounter, place one shard in a clear adjacent square and subtract 3 HP from the parent. If there is no clear square, it cannot split. The shard has 3 HP, no resistance, Stability 0, Speed 4, one-square footprint, and all attributes 0. It cannot split.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Turn the Broadside](../adversary-abilities/turn-the-broadside.md)

## Equipment and limits

Living colony. Ordinary NPC defeat and healing apply. No unlisted immunities or senses. The shard shares the parent’s turn and action budget. Allocate the Main Action, Maneuver and ordinary movement between the bodies. Each spent action or movement allowance goes to one body only. Its only attack is adjacent Shard Sweep for 1/2/3 Physical damage with no Bleeding. The shard has no Reaction. Either body can remain after the other is defeated; if the parent is gone, the shard inherits the same initiative slot.

## Behaviour

It pushes toward a clear flank. Block adjacent space to deny division, or use a different damage type to keep the colony in one body.
