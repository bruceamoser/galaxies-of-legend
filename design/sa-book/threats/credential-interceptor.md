# Credential Interceptor

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Security and synthetics

**Role:** Stops people whose passes fail a checkpoint.

**HP:** 8 · **Speed:** 5 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility +1. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility +1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves on supported surfaces.

## Contact Probe

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** One visible target within 1 square and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 Lightning damage.
- **Standard:** 2 Lightning damage.
- **Strong:** 3 Lightning damage.

## Mark for Review

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** One visible badge within 3 squares.

Record the printed identifier locally. The record does not summon reinforcements or invalidate a pass on its own.

- [Read the Badge](../adversary-abilities/read-the-badge.md)

## Equipment and limits

Nonliving. Ordinary HP healing does not apply. Repairs require access to the damaged component and suitable tools. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

Its amber lens tracks badges before it looks at faces. Request a credential before attacking. An authorized escort clears the alert.
