# White Cinder

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Void entities

**Role:** Drifts toward active emergency lamps.

**HP:** 8 · **Speed:** 5 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 0

**Defence:** Agility +1. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility +1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves on supported surfaces.

## Cinder Flash

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** One visible target within 4 squares and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 Radiant damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Radiant damage.
- **Strong:** 4 Radiant damage. The target gains Blinded 1.

## Follow the Lamp

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** The cinder when its nearest visible emergency lamp goes dark.

Move one square toward another visible lit emergency lamp if a clear route exists. This deals no damage.

- [Collect the Glow](../adversary-abilities/collect-the-glow.md)
- [Cross the Lit Patch](../adversary-abilities/cross-the-lit-patch.md)

## Equipment and limits

Uses the ordinary NPC defeat procedure. No additional immunities or recovery abilities. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

The lamp bleaches white just before a flash. Track the nearest lamp. Shield the lamp or use it to draw the entity away.
