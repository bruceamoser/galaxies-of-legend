# Cold Heap

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** The Slop

**Role:** A white pudding rolls in thick folds, collecting frozen food and tools in its leading edge.

**HP:** 21 · **Speed:** 3 squares · **Stability:** 2 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Fortitude +2. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Ground movement.

## Frozen Roll

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target:** One visible adjacent creature.

- **Weak:** 2 Cold damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Cold damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Cold damage. On a Strong attack, the target gains Freezing 1.

## Raise a Crust

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** One adjacent clear floor square in a freezing area.

Build one low crust in that square. It is difficult terrain and gives cover DR 1 where it intervenes. It melts at the start of the Heap’s next turn or immediately when exposed to active heat.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Freeze the Seam](../adversary-abilities/freeze-the-seam.md)

## Equipment and limits

Living colony. Ordinary NPC defeat and healing apply. No unlisted immunities or senses. Only one crust can exist at a time. Raising another removes the old one. In a warmed compartment the Heap cannot create crust and loses its printed Physical resistance until it returns to freezing conditions.

## Behaviour

It blocks exits instead of chasing fast prey. The freezer controls can take away its barricade and armor.
