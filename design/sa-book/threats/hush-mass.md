# Hush Mass

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Void entities

**Role:** Gathers in chambers after a sudden loss of sound.

**HP:** 15 · **Speed:** 4 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 2

**Defence:** Agility +0. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude +1; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves on supported surfaces.

## Pressure Pulse

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target:** One visible target within 3 squares and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 6 Physical damage. Push the target 1 square directly away from the mass.

## Behaviour

Dust shivers in rings before the next pulse. Stay close to its chamber. A repeated mechanical beat draws it away from a doorway.
