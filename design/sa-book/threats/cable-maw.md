# Cable Maw

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** The Slop

**Role:** A black pudding that raises loops of its body like arms. Live wires glow inside it before it lashes.

**HP:** 18 · **Speed:** 5 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 0

**Defence:** Fortitude 0. **Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude 0; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Climbs supported cable trays at its listed Speed.

## Hook the Hot Tool

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Physical damage. On a Strong attack, if the target holds a powered item, it may release the item into its square. Otherwise pull it 2 squares toward the Maw under normal Stability rules. An unarmed target with no powered item is not pulled.

## Eat the Feed

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** One exposed, energized cable within reach.

Chew through one unarmored branch cable. The equipment solely fed by that branch loses power until bypassed or repaired. This deals no damage to creatures or ship Hull.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Follow the Live Spur](../adversary-abilities/follow-the-live-spur.md)

## Equipment and limits

Living colony. Ordinary NPC defeat and healing apply. No unlisted immunities or senses. A secured or armored branch must be exposed first. A de-energized branch offers no lure. A ship-scale outage requires the SA to identify the actual feed and use the ship rules.

## Behaviour

It favors powered equipment. Put a live sacrificial lead on a clear route to draw it away, or isolate the branch before it gets there.
