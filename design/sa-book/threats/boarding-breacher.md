# Boarding Breacher

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Crews

**Role:** Opens the route for a boarding team.

**HP:** 14 · **Speed:** 5 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility +0. **Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude 0; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves on supported surfaces.

## Breach Maul

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible target within 1 square and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 6 Physical damage. Push the target 1 square directly away from the breacher.

## Brace the Hatch

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** One reachable open hatch.

Hold the hatch open with the maul until the breacher releases it, moves away or is defeated. The breacher cannot use Breach Maul while the weapon is bracing the hatch.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Drop the Barrier](../adversary-abilities/drop-the-barrier.md)
- [Brace for the Ram](../adversary-abilities/brace-for-the-ram.md)

## Equipment and limits

Uses the ordinary NPC defeat procedure. No additional immunities or recovery abilities. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

A braced stance and raised maul identify the hatch under attack. Hold the nearest hatch until allies pass, then withdraw with them.
