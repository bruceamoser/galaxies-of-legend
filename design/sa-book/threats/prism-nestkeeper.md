# Prism Nestkeeper

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Alien ecology

**Role:** Guards a crystal hive in a bright desert ravine.

**HP:** 15 · **Speed:** 4 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 2

**Defence:** Agility +0. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude +1; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves on supported surfaces.

## Focused Glare

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target:** One visible target within 5 squares and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 2 Radiant damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Radiant damage.
- **Strong:** 6 Radiant damage. The target gains Blinded 1.

## Shade the Lens

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** The nestkeeper adjacent to shade.

Move one square into that shade if the square is clear. It cannot use Focused Glare from that position until direct light reaches the lens.

## Equipment and limits

Uses the ordinary NPC defeat procedure. No additional immunities or recovery abilities. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

The lens gathers bright color before focusing into a beam. Stay near the hive. Shade its lens or move out of the ravine to break contact.
