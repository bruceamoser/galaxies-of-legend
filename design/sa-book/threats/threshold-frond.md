# Threshold Frond

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Void entities

**Role:** Reaches through a damaged observation aperture.

**HP:** 14 · **Speed:** 0 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 2

**Defence:** Fortitude +0. **Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude 0; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Anchored in place.

## Frond Sweep

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible target within 3 squares and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 6 Physical damage. Pull the target 1 square toward the aperture along clear ground.

## Withdraw Behind the Rim

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** The frond when it occupies an open observation aperture.

Draw its exposed body behind the aperture rim. It cannot use Frond Sweep until it exposes itself by spending a Maneuver. Whether the crew can still see it depends on their position.

## Equipment and limits

Uses the ordinary NPC defeat procedure. No additional immunities or recovery abilities. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

Condensation pulls inward before the frond reaches through. Remain attached beyond the aperture. Covering the opening cuts its approach.
