# Bilge Walker

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** The Slop

**Role:** Green-black algae spans a frame made from bent pipes. Its legs knock against the deck before the colony enters sight.

**HP:** 19 · **Speed:** 5 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Fortitude 0. **Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude 0; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Ground Speed 5 while framed. Unframed, swim Speed 6 and ground Speed 2.

## Pipe Leg Sweep

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares while framed.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Physical damage. On a Strong attack, push the target 2 squares away under ordinary Stability.

## Shed the Frame

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** Self while framed and adjacent to a reachable drain or flooded square.

Leave the frame behind and move one square into the chosen clear wet space. Lose Physical resistance and Stability from the frame for the rest of the encounter.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Slip Under the Surface](../adversary-abilities/slip-under-the-surface.md)

## Equipment and limits

Living colony. Ordinary NPC defeat and healing apply. No unlisted immunities or senses. Unframed it instead uses an adjacent lash for 1/2/3 Physical damage without a push. It cannot rebuild the frame during the encounter. If the drain is barred, the bars still block its route.

## Behaviour

It drives intruders toward water. Close a drain before attacking if containment matters.
