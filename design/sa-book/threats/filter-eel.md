# Filter Eel

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Station fauna

**Role:** Hides inside flooded recycling channels.

**HP:** 8 · **Speed:** 5 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 0

**Defence:** Agility +1. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility +1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Swims at its listed Speed and cannot move on dry ground.

## Static Snap

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** One visible target within 1 square and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 Lightning damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Lightning damage.
- **Strong:** 4 Lightning damage.

## Slip the Grate

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** The eel when a visible gate starts closing beside it.

Swim one square through the opening if it still fits and the square beyond is flooded and clear.

Uses the ordinary NPC defeat procedure. No additional immunities or recovery abilities. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

Small discharges crackle at the waterline before it lunges. Stay near water. Close the channel grate to cut off pursuit.
