# Mag-Rail Warden

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Security and synthetics

**Role:** Patrols along a fixed overhead track.

**HP:** 11 · **Speed:** 6 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility +1. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility +1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves only along its installed rail.

## Downward Arc

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** One visible target within 5 squares and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 Lightning damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Lightning damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Lightning damage.

Nonliving. Ordinary HP healing does not apply. Repairs require access to the damaged component and suitable tools. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

A moving pool of light shows the arc of its sensor. Stay on the rail. Follow the moving carriage rather than searching off track.
