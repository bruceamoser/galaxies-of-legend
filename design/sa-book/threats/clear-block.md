# Clear Block

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** The Slop

**Role:** A passage-wide transparent slab with tools and scraps hanging inside it. A ripple travels up its face before it rolls forward.

**HP:** 24 · **Speed:** 3 squares · **Stability:** 2 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Fortitude 0. **Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude 0; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** 2 by 2 squares; it can squeeze through a one-square-wide passage, spending 2 movement per square. While squeezed, it occupies two squares in a line.

## Swallow the Opening

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One adjacent creature in front of the Block, with a clear path.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 6 Physical damage. If the Block is holding nobody, it may shift its footprint by one square toward the target as part of this attack. Engulfment is possible only if the shifted footprint includes the entire target and has no other obstruction or occupant. If it shifts, the target remains in its square within the Block and gains maintained Grappled 1.

## Digest

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One creature it has engulfed. The target defends with Fortitude.

- **Weak:** 1 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 4 Physical damage.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Hold the Opening](../adversary-abilities/hold-the-opening.md)

## Equipment and limits

Living colony. Ordinary NPC defeat and healing apply. No unlisted immunities or senses. An engulfed creature can use the ordinary escape procedure or be freed by an adjacent ally using it. On release, place the creature in the nearest clear square it chooses. The Block cannot move while holding a creature. It counts as attached for Hold the Opening only while wedged in a one-square passage. Defeat releases it.

## Behaviour

It follows unobstructed traffic. Narrow passages slow it, and an abandoned load can buy time while it investigates.
