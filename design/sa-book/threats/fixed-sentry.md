# Fixed Sentry

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale threat seed; untested and no calibrated CV.

**Role:** A fixed autonomous defence with a known firing arc and a power connection.

**HP:** 10 · **Speed:** 0 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility -1. **Other attributes:** Agility -1; Reason 0; Knowledge 0. Unlisted attributes are 0; no separate skill bonus.

## Laser burst

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** one visible target within 12 squares and the stated firing arc.

- **Weak:** 2 Thermal damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Thermal damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Thermal damage.

## Equipment and limits

Integrated emitter, local sensor and power feed. Nonliving; no ordinary HP healing. Declare the arc, visible access and a plausible shutdown route when placing it.

## Behaviour

Acts once on its turn when autonomous. If operated remotely, its shot instead uses the operator's Main Action; never both. Blocked sight prevents an attack. Disabling the actual power feed stops it without a hidden last shot.
