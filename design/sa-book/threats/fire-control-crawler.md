# Fire Control Crawler

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Security and synthetics

**Role:** Responds to heat alarms in service tunnels.

**HP:** 8 · **Speed:** 5 squares · **Stability:** 0 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility +1. **Attributes:** Brawn 0; Fortitude 0; Agility +1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves on supported surfaces.

## Coolant Jet

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target:** One visible target within 3 squares and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 Cold damage.
- **Standard:** 2 Cold damage.
- **Strong:** 4 Cold damage. The target gains Freezing 1.

## Reorient Nozzle

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** One visible heat source within 3 squares.

Turn the nozzle toward that source. This can reveal the spray direction to observers and deals no damage.

- [Move to Service Mark](../adversary-abilities/move-to-service-mark.md)
- [Vent the Steam](../adversary-abilities/vent-the-steam.md)

## Equipment and limits

Nonliving. Ordinary HP healing does not apply. Repairs require access to the damaged component and suitable tools. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

Frost on the nozzle shows the line it is about to spray. Follow the hottest visible source. Its manual service switch stops the spray.
