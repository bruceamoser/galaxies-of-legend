# Hull Grazer

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** The Slop

**Role:** Dark lobes anchor and release in sequence on a ship’s exterior. Its rear edge trails peeled flakes of coating.

**HP:** 23 · **Speed:** 4 squares · **Stability:** 2 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Fortitude 0. **Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude 0; Agility -1; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** 2 by 1 squares. Climbs exterior surfaces at its listed Speed; it does not need air and cannot move across unsupported open space.

## Pin to the Plate

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible adjacent target touching the same hull or reachable tether.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 6 Physical damage. On a Strong attack, the target gains maintained Grappled 1 if the Grazer is holding nobody.

## Transfer to Bait

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** One adjacent coated plate large enough to support its footprint.

Release any captive and move onto the plate if it is unobstructed and the plate offers fresher coating than the surface the Grazer occupies.

## Shared adversary abilities

- [Hold the Opening](../adversary-abilities/hold-the-opening.md)

## Equipment and limits

Living colony. Ordinary NPC defeat and healing apply. No unlisted immunities or senses. The Grazer cannot hold more than one creature. Escape and release follow ordinary grapple rules. Peeling coating does not itself deal Hull damage. If the SA places it over an exposed fitting, state the threatened fitting and use the Starship Manual for any resulting system incident.

## Behaviour

Show the crew flaking coating and the lobe that reaches for a boot. A prepared salvage plate can separate it from the ship without a fight.
