# Cargo Clamp Drone

**Status:** Playtest proposal - personal-scale adversary, untested. CV uncalibrated.

**Family:** Security and synthetics

**Role:** Secures loose loads when a bay alarm sounds.

**HP:** 11 · **Speed:** 4 squares · **Stability:** 1 · **Physical resistance:** 1

**Defence:** Agility +0. **Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude 0; Agility 0; Guile 0; Knowledge 0; Reason 0.

**Movement and footprint:** One square. Moves on supported surfaces.

## Clamp Strike

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target:** One visible target within 1 square and with a clear path for the attack.

- **Weak:** 2 Physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Physical damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Physical damage. The target falls Prone.

## Drop the Load

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target:** A load held by the drone when a visible obstruction stops its movement.

Open the clamp immediately. Apply the established falling cargo hazard only if the load actually falls into the declared danger area.

## Equipment and limits

Nonliving. Ordinary HP healing does not apply. Repairs require access to the damaged component and suitable tools. The listed attack requires the named implement or body part to be functional. Apply ordinary condition timing and forced movement rules.

## Behaviour

Yellow paint marks where its clamps can reach. Prioritize moving objects in marked lanes. A loading pause disables automatic clamping.
