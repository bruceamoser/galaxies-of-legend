# Galaxies of Legend Sector Architect's Book

Base SA volume for adjudication, sectors and factions, situations, encounters, hazards, rewards, campaigns and review records. It includes 67 personal-scale adversaries across six families and four printable worksheets. Threat values, hazard fixtures and encounter guidance are untested proposals; no calibrated GOL CV or starter mission is claimed.

- `manuscript/`: nine authored teaching chapters.
- `encounters/`: six worked scenarios with reproducible roster totals. Run `python3 design/sa-book/cv.py` to check the provisional comparisons.
- `threats/`: 67 editable Markdown profiles with explicit actions, attributes, damage, equipment and limits.
- `adversary-abilities/`: canonical reusable SA abilities. The publisher inlines assigned rules in each profile and prints the library at the end of Chapter 6.
- `companions/`: personal drones, software sprites and hired companions, printed in Appendix A. The Player’s Book owns the common command procedure.
- `sheets.typ`: sector, scene, encounter and session-review records.
- `book.typ`, `cover.typ`: assembly entry points regenerated by the companion publisher.

Build the Player's Book first, then `python3 design/publishing/build_companions.py sa-book`. The PDF is `output/galaxies-of-legend-sa-book.pdf`. `generated/` and `output/` are disposable. The complete personal harm foundation remains in Player's Book Chapter 7. Ship mechanics and ship sheets belong to the separate Starship Manual.
