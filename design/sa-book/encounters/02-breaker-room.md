# Breaker Room

**Test party levels:** 1, 1, 1

**Comparison budget:** standard

- 1 × [Fixed Sentry](../threats/fixed-sentry.md)
- 1 × [Patrol Drone](../threats/patrol-drone.md)
- 1 × [Filter Eel](../threats/filter-eel.md)

**Objective:** Isolate a failed pump before the flooded berth loses its dry access route.

**Set up:** An 8 by 8 square room has a flooded channel along the north edge. Put the eel in that channel, the sentry at the northeast corner aimed down the east aisle, and the patrol drone 4 squares from the southwest entry. The breaker is 6 squares from the entry and reachable by either a short dry path in the sentry’s arc or a longer route by the channel. A sealed pump housing provides cover DR 2 where it actually blocks an attack.

**Run it:** Show the sentry’s painted arc and the eel’s discharges in the water. The sentry cannot fire outside its arc. The eel stays in the water. An accessible, working breaker shuts down the sentry and isolates the pump, though the drone has its own power. A crew that closes the channel grate can block the eel’s approach. Pulling the breaker completes the objective. No actor automatically joins through a door that the crew has sealed.

**Calibration use:** Compare a direct crossing to a route that avoids the arc. Record how many nominal threats can actually attack in the same round.
