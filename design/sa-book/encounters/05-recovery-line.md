# Recovery Line

**Test party levels:** 3, 3, 3, 3

**Comparison budget:** hard

- 2 × [Contract Guard](../threats/contract-guard.md)
- 2 × [Frontier Raider](../threats/frontier-raider.md)
- 1 × [Field Medic](../threats/field-medic.md)

**Objective:** Escort a survey witness to the departure hatch while a rival team tries to delay them.

**Set up:** Use a 14 by 8 square warehouse. Put the crew and witness by the west ramp. Guards start 6 squares east in separate lanes. The medic starts 2 squares behind one guard with a full medkit. The departure hatch is at the east edge. Crates give cover DR 1 where they intercept a line. The two raiders wait at the north service door.

**Run it:** The guards want the witness to stay until a ship clears the berth. A visible dispatch light warns that reinforcements are coming. The raiders enter at the end of round two if the service door is open and their caller can reach them. A player can close the door with its working panel or cut the caller’s established link. The medic chooses among movement, care and self-preservation and pays the printed HP and medkit costs. The rival team yields if the witness reaches safety or its departure authorization is withdrawn.

**Calibration use:** Test with and without the medic, keeping the same initial positions. Record effective HP restored, medic HP spent, whether treatment displaced pressure from other actions, and whether the reinforcements arrived. The subtotal excludes the medic and therefore cannot classify the full encounter.
