# Signal Cut

**Test party levels:** 3, 3, 3, 3

**Comparison budget:** standard

- 1 × [White Cinder](../threats/white-cinder.md)
- 1 × [Echo Knot](../threats/echo-knot.md)
- 1 × [Hush Mass](../threats/hush-mass.md)
- 1 × [Wrong-Window Figure](../threats/wrong-window-figure.md)

**Objective:** Pull the relay’s recording cassette and leave before the next scheduled transmission.

**Set up:** Two 6 by 6 square chambers share a central doorway. Start the crew in the west chamber beside the entrance and emergency lamp switch. Put the cassette and viewport in the east chamber, with the figure next to the viewport. Put the cinder beside the west lamp, the knot next to an open receiver 3 squares from the crew, and the mass at the east wall. The viewport has a reachable opaque shutter control.

**Run it:** Demonstrate each entity’s cue before it can act on the crew. A lamp can redirect the cinder, muting the receiver changes what draws the knot, and closing the shutter cuts the figure’s line. The mass can be drawn away from the cassette by an established mechanical beat. At the end of round four the transmission begins. It changes the audio and light cues, giving the SA a new situation to describe, without granting any free attack.

**Calibration use:** Track how often Dazed or Blinded prevents a declared action. Compare a run with the crews told the cues explicitly to a run where they have to investigate visible evidence. The seed uses one target and omits the timing of combined condition riders.
