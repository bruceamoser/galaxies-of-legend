# Berth Hold

**Test party levels:** 1, 1, 1, 1

**Comparison budget:** standard

- 4 × [Dock Watch](../threats/dock-watch.md)

**Objective:** Retrieve a sealed cargo box from a berth placed on hold before a launch window closes.

**Set up:** Use a 12 by 8 square dock. The crew starts at the west entry. Put two watch officers 6 squares east at a painted checkpoint, one 3 squares north of them behind a waist-high cargo stack, and one by the east exit. The box is 2 squares inside the checkpoint. The south service aisle is open but visible from the checkpoint. Use cover DR 1 for the cargo stack where it intervenes.

**Run it:** The watch first asks for authorization. They keep the box in place and use their ordinary actions if attacked. The officer at the east exit holds position unless the box moves. Show the berth-hold notice and the name of the official who signed it. A confirmed release order ends opposition. If the party takes the box out either exit, the encounter ends even if the watch remains capable of fighting.

**Calibration use:** This roster defines the level-1 comparison anchor. Run an open-dock version with the stack removed and keep the same starting positions. Record how much damage prevention and movement the stack adds.
