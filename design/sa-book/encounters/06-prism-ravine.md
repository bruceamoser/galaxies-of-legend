# Prism Ravine

**Test party levels:** 7, 7, 7, 7

**Comparison budget:** standard

- 2 × [Prism Nestkeeper](../threats/prism-nestkeeper.md)
- 2 × [Saltglass Herdling](../threats/saltglass-herdling.md)
- 1 × [Threshold Frond](../threats/threshold-frond.md)

**Objective:** Recover a probe from a marked ledge without leaving an explorer beyond the ravine exit.

**Set up:** Use a 14 by 10 square ravine. The party enters at the west end, the probe rests 9 squares east, and two nestkeepers start at separate bright rock faces 4 squares apart. The herd occupies the southern feeding flat. Place the frond in a broken observation aperture 2 squares beyond the probe. A shaded ledge and an open service track both reach the probe. Mark the ledge’s narrow points and the aperture’s reach.

**Run it:** Show the lens color and the herd’s scrape marks. Shade the lens to interfere with the nestkeepers’ glare. A wide approach leaves the herd a way out. Covering the aperture stops the frond from reaching through it. The threats stay near their territories rather than coordinate as a single faction. Recovering the probe and reaching the exit ends the scene.

**Calibration use:** Run once with a clear shaded route and once with that route partially obstructed. Record how much of the paper roster can engage at once and whether blindness or forced movement has more impact than HP damage.
