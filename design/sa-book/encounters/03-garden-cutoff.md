# Garden Cutoff

**Test party levels:** 1, 1, 3, 3

**Comparison budget:** standard

- 2 × [Heatpipe Crab](../threats/heatpipe-crab.md)
- 1 × [Spore Lantern](../threats/spore-lantern.md)
- 1 × [Industrial Loader](../threats/industrial-loader.md)

**Objective:** Retrieve a sample rack from a cultivation bay without disturbing the nursery.

**Set up:** Put the crew at the west hatch of a 10 by 8 square bay. The rack is 7 squares east. Each crab starts by a hot valve on opposite sides of the central aisle. The rooted lantern is 2 squares south of the rack. The loader blocks the north lane 5 squares from the hatch. Mark a maintenance route around the valves and a shutter control near the entrance.

**Run it:** The crabs hold their valves. The lantern responds to disturbance near its stalk. The loader follows its marked lane and stops if the crew reaches the ordinary loading pause at its control point. Show the warning colors, hot pipes and lane paint before the crew moves. Diverting warmth, leaving space around the lantern and pausing the loader can open a route to the rack without combat.

**Calibration use:** Use the same level mix with and without the loader active. Record how much pressure comes from blocking the route rather than damage. The fixed roster total includes the loader in the active version.
