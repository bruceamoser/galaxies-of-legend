# Secure Traverse

*You find the foothold that lets everyone else follow.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - starter coverage, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One physical crossing or ascent within reach

Secure a passage with a rope or suitable handholds. The SA sets progress, usually 3.

- **Weak:** 1 progress; leave yourself exposed to one declared hazard.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; the next ally using this passage gains a Boon on their crossing check.

**Duration:** The passage remains usable until conditions change; Boon lasts one crossing.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a rope or suitable handholds

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Athletics

**Limits:** One instance of this card's benefit per target or task; repeat only for new progress, a new trigger or a changed situation. Pay the printed action and time.
