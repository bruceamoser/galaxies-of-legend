# Reconstruct the Injury

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One willing patient within reach and their clinical record; one hour

Determine how an injury occurred and what earlier treatment addressed. Apply ordinary treatment rules separately.

- **Weak:** Identify one likely source of the injury.
- **Standard:** Identify the likely source and which recorded wound it produced.
- **Strong:** Also identify a clue that distinguishes it from a similar source.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Medic; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Medic

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
