# Black Market Contact

*You ask for something that is not on the manifest and wait to see who answers.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One settlement or station; one hour

Name the item, service or person you are looking for. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; the contact wants a favour or a higher price.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; you find a seller or broker with the thing you need.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; the contact also throws in one useful rumour about the local power structure.

**Duration:** The contact and rumour persist until circumstances change.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill / Social

**Tags:** Social / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** black-market-contact
