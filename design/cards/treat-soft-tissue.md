# Treat Soft Tissue

*You wash away the grit and work stitch by stitch, giving torn flesh the chance to knit.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Time:** One hour.

**Target / range / shape:** One wound on yourself or a willing patient listing Soft Tissue treatment.

Make a Knowledge treatment roll.

- **Weak:** Add 1 recovery progress.
- **Standard:** Add 2 recovery progress.
- **Strong:** Add 3 recovery progress.

**Duration:** Recovery progress remains with that wound; at its RV remove only the printed treatable effects. The scar still counts.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No equipment is granted. Provide any access and materials required by the wound's remedy; a med bay or medkit does not grant this capability.

**Limits:** One attempt per wound per day across all healers. Each attempt takes the full hour, not one combat action. No group treatment; no regrowth or reversal of Last Words.
