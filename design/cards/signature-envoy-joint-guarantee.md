# Joint Guarantee

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Two willing parties with authority to make a limited agreement; one hour

Write a mutual commitment with named witnesses.

- **Weak:** Record the condition holding up agreement.
- **Standard:** Both accept a limited obligation and a clear exit condition.
- **Strong:** They also name who will resolve disagreements.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Envoy

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
