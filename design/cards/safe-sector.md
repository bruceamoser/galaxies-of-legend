# Safe Sector

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self

When shown the position of friendly crew and the limits of your mounted weapon, you can mark the overlapping no-fire sector on its controls if the interface supports it.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Gunnery / Passive

**Limits:** Applies only under the circumstances stated above.
