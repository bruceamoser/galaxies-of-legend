# Shipboard Familiarity

*You have spent enough time in hulls to know which sound means trouble.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self

Once per scene, gain a Boon on one Piloting, Engineering or Navigation check made aboard a ship or station.

**Duration:** One scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Skill / Ship

**Tags:** Ship / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** shipboard-familiarity
