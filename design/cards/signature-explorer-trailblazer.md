# Trailblazer

*You find the path and make it look as if it was always there.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Self and up to four allies

Lead the group through difficult terrain or a hazard.

- **Weak:** the group moves at half Speed; one ally is exposed.
- **Standard:** the group moves normally and avoids the hazard.
- **Strong:** the group moves normally, avoids the hazard and gains a Boon on the next check in the area.

**Duration:** The journey.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Explorer; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Exploration / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-explorer-trailblazer
