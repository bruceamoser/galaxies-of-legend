# Breakthrough

*You stop looking at the problem the way it was presented.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One problem, code, alien system or scientific obstacle

Attempt a decisive insight. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; identify the missing piece.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; solve the immediate problem.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; solve it and grant every ally a Boon on their next related check.

**Duration:** The solution persists; the Boon lasts until used.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Scientist; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Utility / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-scientist-breakthrough
