# Power Strike

*You put your whole body behind the blow and let the ground take the rest.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature

Make a melee attack.

- **Weak:** 3 Kinetic damage.
- **Standard:** 5 Kinetic damage.
- **Strong:** 7 Kinetic damage; push the target 1 square.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a melee weapon

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Arms 1

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Tags:** Attack

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** power-strike
