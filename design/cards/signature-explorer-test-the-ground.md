# Test the Ground

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One reachable surface or foothold

Test the surface before committing your weight.

- **Weak:** Learn whether it will hold you briefly.
- **Standard:** Identify a safe foothold or confirm none exists.
- **Strong:** Also identify a route across for a second traveler.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.
