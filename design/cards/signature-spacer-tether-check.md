# Tether Check

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One reachable tether and anchor

Inspect the attachment before a crew member leaves a hatch.

- **Weak:** Identify visible damage to the tether.
- **Standard:** Confirm whether the attachment will hold its rated load.
- **Strong:** Also identify a second usable anchor if available.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Spacer; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Spacer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
