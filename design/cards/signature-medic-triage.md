# Triage

*You decide who needs you first and make that decision visible to everyone.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Up to two wounded creatures within reach

**Healing cost:** Pay 1 HP before rolling, bypassing resistance; you must remain above 0 HP. Requires a free hand. Apply Life resistance to HP restored, minimum 1 where HP is missing, capped by maximum HP. This emergency-care card may stabilize a Dying recipient as its outcomes state. It never removes a scar, restores Grit or reverses Last Words. Consume one medkit use per application.

Treat the most urgent wound first.

- **Weak:** stabilise one creature at 0 HP.
- **Standard:** stabilise one creature and restore 2 HP to another.
- **Strong:** stabilise one creature, restore 4 HP to another, and remove Bleeding 1 from both.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a medkit

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Medic; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Recovery / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-medic-triage
