# Preserve the Airway

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent patient whose face is about to be covered during transport; free hand

Keep the obstruction off the patient’s face while adjacent and holding it clear. This does not treat Dying or restore HP.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 3

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Medicine / Reaction

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
