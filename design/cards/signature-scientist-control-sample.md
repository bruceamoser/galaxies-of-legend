# Control Sample

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Two comparable accessible samples; ten minutes

Separate one control from the experimental sample and document their conditions.

- **Weak:** Identify one uncontrolled difference.
- **Standard:** Establish a usable control if the samples permit it.
- **Strong:** Also identify a second variable to hold constant.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.
