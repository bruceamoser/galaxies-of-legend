# Borrowed Authority

*You make the room expect the person you are pretending to be.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - new signature, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One willing-to-listen NPC within conversation range

Request one reversible, plausible procedural concession: a meeting, a pause or a record lookup. The SA states what evidence could support it; this is not mind control.

- **Weak:** 1 progress toward a 3-progress concession; the NPC verifies your claim.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; the NPC also names the person responsible for the decision.

**Duration:** The current conversation; impossible or dangerous requests remain impossible.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires supporting evidence, a cover identity or a credible introduction

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Con Artist; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Con Artist

**Limits:** Do not stack multiple instances of the same benefit; each granted Boon applies once. Pay the action and any stated time.
