# Hold the Line

*You step into the path of the blow and make the line hold.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** One ally within 4 squares who is hit by an attack

Reduce the damage by 2. On a Strong result, reduce it by 4 and the ally gains a Boon on their next attack.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Defense / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-soldier-hold-the-line
