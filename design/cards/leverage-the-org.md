# Leverage the Org

*You know which form opens which door, and which clerk is tired enough to help.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One institution, record office or corporate service; one hour

State the access, record or service you want. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3) and tells you what a failure will cost.

- **Weak:** 1 progress; the request is logged and someone notices.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; you also learn the name of one person who can help.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; you find a shortcut that saves an hour or avoids one intermediary.

**Duration:** The access or progress persists for the stated task.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Influence 1

**Type:** Skill / Social

**Tags:** Social / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** leverage-the-org
