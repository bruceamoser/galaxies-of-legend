# Plot the Dead Stick

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One craft with a known position and velocity and access to navigation data; ten minutes

Calculate where the craft can coast on its current trajectory.

- **Weak:** Estimate the next point of closest approach.
- **Standard:** Plot the coast and identify a reachable interception point if available.
- **Strong:** Also identify the smallest correction needed to reach a second point if feasible.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Spacer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
