# Jam Release

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One jammed unpowered hatch or mechanism within reach

Free a mechanism whose controls you can physically reach. This does not defeat a lock.

- **Weak:** Free it enough for one use, after which it jams again.
- **Standard:** Free it for the scene.
- **Strong:** Free it for the scene and identify what must be replaced to prevent another jam.

**Duration:** By outcome

**Equipment / acquisition:** Toolkit

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Technician; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Technician

**Limits:** Pay the action and stated time. This card grants no improvement to installed system dice.
