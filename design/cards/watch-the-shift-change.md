# Find an Opening

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One guarded or monitored route you can observe

Find a usable opening in its surveillance. At 3 progress name an observed path and window or confirm that the observed route provides neither. This grants no Hidden condition or permission to enter.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

**Duration:** Progress remains on the declared task until it resolves or circumstances invalidate it.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires appropriate access and any equipment named in the effect.
