# Power Routing

*You move power from the life support someone is not using to the gun someone is.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One ship system within reach

Move one step of power between two ship systems.

- **Weak:** shift one step; the donor system steps down at the end of the scene.
- **Standard:** shift one step for the scene.
- **Strong:** shift two steps, split as you choose.

**Duration:** One scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires access to the power plant

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Spacer; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Technical / Ship

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
