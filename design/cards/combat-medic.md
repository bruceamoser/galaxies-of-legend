# Combat Medic

*You work through the noise because stopping is not an option the patient can afford.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One wounded creature within reach

Provide emergency care under fire.

- **Weak:** restore 3 HP.
- **Standard:** restore 6 HP and remove Bleeding 1.
- **Strong:** restore 9 HP, remove Bleeding 1 and grant a Boon to the patient's next Fortitude check.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a medkit

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 2

**Type:** Skill / Recovery

**Tags:** Recovery / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** combat-medic
