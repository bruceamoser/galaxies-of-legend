# Brace the Door

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** One open doorway you occupy

Set your footing to hold the opening until your next turn.

- **Weak:** Gain a Boon on your next check to resist being moved through it.
- **Standard:** Gain that Boon and give an ally adjacent to you a Boon on their next check to hold position there.
- **Strong:** As Standard. Apply the ally benefit to two adjacent allies.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Soldier

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.

**ID:** signature-soldier-brace-the-door
