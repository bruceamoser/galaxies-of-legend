# Cache Site

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One spot you can inspect; ten minutes

Find somewhere to conceal a portable supply cache.

- **Weak:** Find a dry spot with an obvious approach.
- **Standard:** Find a concealed site and leave a retrieval cue for your crew.
- **Strong:** Also identify a second approach to the cache.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Explorer; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Explorer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
