# Flight Recorder Review

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible flight recorder; ten minutes

Reconstruct the last recorded maneuver from the available data.

- **Weak:** Identify its start and end times.
- **Standard:** Identify its timing and the inputs used.
- **Strong:** Also identify a recorded deviation from the intended course.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Spacer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
