# Jury-Rig

*You strip a dead panel, borrow a fuse, and make the machine believe it is still alive.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One broken device or system within reach; 10 minutes

Improvise a repair.

- **Weak:** the device works for one action, then fails with a visible flaw.
- **Standard:** the device works for the scene.
- **Strong:** the device works until properly repaired and grants a Boon to its next use.

**Duration:** One action, one scene, or until repaired, by result.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a toolkit or suitable parts

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Engineering 1

**Type:** Skill / Technical

**Tags:** Technical / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** jury-rig
