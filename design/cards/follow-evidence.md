# Follow Evidence

*You separate the evidence from the explanation someone wants you to believe.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - starter coverage, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible scene, record or witness; ten minutes

Investigate a stated question. The SA sets progress, usually 3. You can only find evidence that exists and is accessible.

- **Weak:** 1 progress; reveal your interest to someone involved.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; identify one verifiable lead.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; identify a lead and one way to test its reliability.

**Duration:** The evidence persists unless destroyed or contradicted.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires access to the evidence or a willing witness

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Investigation 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Investigation

**Limits:** One instance of this card's benefit per target or task; repeat only for new progress, a new trigger or a changed situation. Pay the printed action and time.
