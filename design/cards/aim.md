# Aim

*You slow your breathing and let the sight settle where the shot will go.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self

Gain a Boon on your next ranged attack this turn. If you move before attacking, the Boon is lost.

**Duration:** End of turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 1

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Tags:** Attack / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** aim
