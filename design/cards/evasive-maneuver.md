# Evasive Manoeuvre

*You throw the ship sideways and trust the hull to argue with physics later.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one ship you are piloting

When attacked, gain +1 DR against that attack. On a Strong result, also move 1 range band after the attack.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a vehicle or ship you are piloting

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Piloting 1

**Type:** Skill / Defense

**Tags:** Defense / Ship

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** evasive-maneuver
