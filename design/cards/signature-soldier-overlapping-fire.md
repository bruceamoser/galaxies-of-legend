# Overlapping Fire

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within weapon range and one willing ally who can see it

Fire a ready weapon and call where your ally should aim.

- **Weak:** Deal 3 weapon damage.
- **Standard:** Deal 4 weapon damage and grant the ally a Boon on their next attack against that creature this scene.
- **Strong:** Deal 5 weapon damage and grant that Boon.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready weapon of the stated kind.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Soldier

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.

**ID:** signature-soldier-overlapping-fire
