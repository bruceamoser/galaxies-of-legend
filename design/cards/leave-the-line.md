# Leave the Line

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when a visible moving camera begins to sweep your position

Move one square into available cover if you can reach it before the camera has a clear view. Normal movement costs apply.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 3

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Subterfuge / Reaction

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
