# Make the Introduction

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally and a contact who can hear you

Introduce the ally through a concrete shared concern. Give the ally 1 Boon on their next Guile or Reason check this scene to seek the contact’s cooperation.

**Duration:** As stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Influence 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Influence / Maneuver

**Limits:** Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** make-the-introduction
