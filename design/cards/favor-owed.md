# Favour Owed

*You call in a debt and hope the person who owes it still remembers the terms.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One contact who owes you a favour

Ask for a favour within the contact's power. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; the contact wants the favour returned immediately.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; the contact agrees within their means.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; the contact agrees and offers one additional piece of local information.

**Duration:** The favour and information persist until used or circumstances change.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill / Social

**Tags:** Social / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** favor-owed
