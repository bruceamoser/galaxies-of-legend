# Controlled Disclosure

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One listener about to disclose your cover identity in a conversation you can hear

Reveal a true fact about a separate issue to give them a reason to delay.

- **Weak:** They agree to hear one sentence before speaking.
- **Standard:** They delay disclosure until the conversation ends.
- **Strong:** They also tell you privately what prompted the disclosure.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Con Artist; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Con Artist

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
