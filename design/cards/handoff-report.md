# Handoff Report

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing clinician within speaking distance and a patient you assessed this scene

Give the clinician a Boon on their next roll this scene to assess that patient. This does not affect treatment or HP restoration.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Medicine / Maneuver

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
