# Called Shot

*You aim at the seam, the intake or the power line and make the machine regret its design.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible vehicle, ship or creature with a system within 10 squares

Make a ranged attack against a specific system or weak point.

- **Weak:** 3 Kinetic damage; no system effect.
- **Standard:** 5 Kinetic damage; step the targeted system's die down one size.
- **Strong:** 7 Kinetic damage; step the system down one size and it cannot be used next round.

**Duration:** Instant; system damage persists until repaired.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 2

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Tags:** Attack / Technical

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** called-shot
