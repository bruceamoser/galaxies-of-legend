# Last Light

*You refuse to let the dark take this one yet.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One creature within reach that has just been reduced to 0 HP

Bring the creature back from the edge.

- **Weak:** the creature stabilises at 0 HP.
- **Standard:** the creature returns to 1 HP.
- **Strong:** the creature returns to 3 HP and gains a Boon on its next death roll this scene.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a medkit

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Medic; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Recovery / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-medic-last-light
