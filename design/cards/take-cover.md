# Take Cover

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self while you have cover from a visible threat

Settle behind your existing cover. Gain 1 Boon on your next defense against an attack from that threat before the start of your next turn, while the same barrier still gives you cover.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Tags:** Maneuver

**Limits:** Spend the listed action and observe any stated limit.
