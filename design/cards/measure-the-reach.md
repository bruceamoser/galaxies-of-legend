# Probe the Guard

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One visible opponent within reach of your ready melee weapon

Make a melee attack to drive the opponent out of position. Use the weapon’s damage type.

- **Weak:** 2 damage.
- **Standard:** 3 damage and push the target 1 square.
- **Strong:** 4 damage and push the target 2 squares.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready melee weapon

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Arms 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Arms / Main Action

**Limits:** Spend the listed action. Resolve the printed result once.
