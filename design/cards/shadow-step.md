# Shadow Step

*You move when the light changes and stop being where the room expects you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Self

Move up to your Speed and make a Stealth check to hide if you end in cover or dim light. On a Strong result, you may move 2 additional squares.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill / Movement

**Tags:** Movement / Stealth

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** shadow-step
