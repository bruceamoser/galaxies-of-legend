# Convert an Action

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

## Rules

**Action:** Action conversion · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self

Spend your Main Action to gain a Maneuver, or spend your Maneuver to gain another movement allowance. You may convert the Maneuver gained from your Main Action.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Tags:** Action conversion

**Limits:** Spend the listed action and observe any stated limit.
