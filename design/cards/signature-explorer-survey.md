# Survey

*You build a picture of the place before anyone else steps into it.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One area, structure or route within sight

Survey the area. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; one entrance or hazard.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; entrances, hazards and one useful feature.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; also find a hidden route or cache.

**Duration:** The survey persists.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Explorer; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Exploration / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-explorer-survey
