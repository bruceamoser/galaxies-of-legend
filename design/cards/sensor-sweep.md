# Sensor Sweep

*You send a pulse into the dark and listen to what comes back changed.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One vessel, structure or area within sensor range

Scan the target. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; detect one active system or life sign.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; identify systems, occupants and one weakness.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; also learn one current order, route or secret the target is trying to keep.

**Duration:** The scan remains valid for the scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a sensor pack or ship sensors

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Science 1

**Type:** Skill / Technical

**Tags:** Technical / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
