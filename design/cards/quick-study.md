# Quick Study

*You have read enough to know which page to open.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One topic, record or device you can examine

Recall or research a fact. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; a partial or outdated fact.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; a reliable answer.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; a precise answer and one related lead.

**Duration:** The information persists.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
