# Fabricate a Coupling

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Two devices within reach with incompatible service connections; one uninterrupted hour

Make a removable adapter for one named signal or fluid connection within both devices’ rated limits.

- **Weak:** Carry the connection for a single test.
- **Standard:** Carry the connection for one scene.
- **Strong:** Carry the connection until the adapter is removed or damaged.

**Duration:** By outcome

**Equipment / acquisition:** Workshop, toolkit and suitable stock

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Technician; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Technician

**Limits:** Pay the action and stated time. This card grants no improvement to installed system dice.
