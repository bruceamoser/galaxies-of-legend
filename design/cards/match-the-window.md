# Match the Window

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One cooperating craft in comms and a craft you operate

Agree on a common heading and closing limit for a transfer. Give the other pilot a Boon on their next roll this scene to match that approach.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Piloting 3

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Piloting / Maneuver

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** match-the-window
