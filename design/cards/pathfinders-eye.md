# Pathfinder's Eye

*You read the ground for the story it tells about water, weather and what passed before you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One landscape, structure or trail; 10 minutes

Survey the route ahead. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; spot one obvious hazard.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; find a safe path or a useful landmark.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; find a path that avoids the hazard entirely and note one hidden feature.

**Duration:** The route knowledge persists for the journey.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Navigation 1

**Type:** Skill / Exploration

**Tags:** Exploration / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** pathfinders-eye
