# Call the Transfer

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally you can communicate with before they roll to move a patient within your sight

Give the ally 1 Boon on their Brawn or Agility check to move the patient. Name the safe point you are guiding them toward. This restores no HP.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Medic; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Medic

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.

**ID:** signature-medic-call-the-transfer
