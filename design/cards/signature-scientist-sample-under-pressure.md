# Sample Under Pressure

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One sample you are holding when its container begins to fail

Preserve what you can using a ready secondary container.

- **Weak:** Preserve enough to identify its broad category.
- **Standard:** Preserve a usable sample.
- **Strong:** Preserve the sample and its original label.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Scientist; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Scientist

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
