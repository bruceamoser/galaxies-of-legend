# Quiet Hinge

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One unlocked hinged door within reach

Control the hinge and latch to reduce noise as you open it.

- **Weak:** Crack the door far enough to look through.
- **Standard:** Open it far enough for one person to pass without audible hinge noise nearby.
- **Strong:** Also close it quietly after passing.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Operator; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Operator

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
