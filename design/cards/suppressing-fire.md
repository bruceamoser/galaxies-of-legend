# Suppressing Fire

*You fill the space they need to cross and make them choose between cover and progress.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One 2-square-wide area within 8 squares

Spray the area with fire. Each creature in the area makes a defense roll against your attack.

- **Weak:** 2 Kinetic damage to each target.
- **Standard:** 3 Kinetic damage; Slowed 1 until the end of their next turn.
- **Strong:** 4 Kinetic damage; Slowed 1 and the area remains dangerous until your next turn.

**Duration:** Instant; the area lasts until your next turn on a Strong result.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon and ammunition

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 1

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Tags:** Attack / Control

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** suppressing-fire
