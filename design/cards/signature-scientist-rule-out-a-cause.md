# Rule Out a Cause

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One observed phenomenon and two testable explanations; ten minutes

Perform a test whose results can distinguish the explanations.

- **Weak:** Identify a confounding factor.
- **Standard:** Rule out one explanation if the result supports it.
- **Strong:** Also identify a prediction that could independently confirm the remaining explanation.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.
