# Unbroken

*You have been hit before. You are still standing.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self

Once per scene, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, remain at 1 HP instead. You may also ignore one wound penalty until the end of your next turn.

**Duration:** One scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Defense / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-soldier-unbroken
