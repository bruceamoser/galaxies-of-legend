# Reconstruct the Route

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible scene or set of records with evidence of a creature’s passage

Reconstruct a specific movement or sequence. At 3 progress establish the route or the last supported location. Missing evidence remains missing.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

**Duration:** Progress remains on the declared task until it resolves or circumstances invalidate it.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires appropriate access and any equipment named in the effect.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Investigation 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Investigation / Main Action

**Limits:** Spend the listed action. Resolve the printed result once.

**ID:** reconstruct-the-route
