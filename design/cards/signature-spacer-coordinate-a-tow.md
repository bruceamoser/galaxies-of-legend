# Coordinate a Tow

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Two cooperating craft within comms with compatible tow hardware; ten minutes

Plan how to take a disabled craft under tow without overloading the attachment.

- **Weak:** Identify the safe loading limit.
- **Standard:** Establish a sequence and grant a Boon on the first check to connect the tow.
- **Strong:** Also grant a Boon on the first check to disengage during the same operation.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Spacer; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Spacer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
