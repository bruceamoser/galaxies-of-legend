# Fire Discipline

*You turn a scattered exchange into a controlled volume of fire.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Up to two visible creatures within 10 squares

Make one ranged attack against each target.

- **Weak:** 3 Kinetic damage to each.
- **Standard:** 5 Kinetic damage to each.
- **Strong:** 7 Kinetic damage to each; one target is Slowed 1.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Attack / Control

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-soldier-fire-discipline
