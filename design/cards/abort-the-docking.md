# Abort the Docking

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when a visible obstruction enters your docking path before contact

Cancel the docking input. Existing velocity still applies and may require further action.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Piloting 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Piloting / Reaction

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** abort-the-docking
