# Appraise the Deal

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible offer, consignment, or service contract

Determine the practical cost or fair local value. At 3 progress account for visible fees and identify the largest unresolved term. The seller sets their own price.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

**Duration:** Progress remains on the declared task until it resolves or circumstances invalidate it.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires appropriate access and any equipment named in the effect.

**Tags:** Trade / Main Action

**Limits:** Spend the listed action. Resolve the printed result once.
