# Cooling Interval

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Two willing parties about to end a negotiation you are attending

Offer a short break with an agreed time to resume.

- **Weak:** One party tells you why they are leaving.
- **Standard:** Both agree to return once this scene if safe.
- **Strong:** They also agree what topic to address first.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Envoy

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
