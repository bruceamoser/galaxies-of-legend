# Count the Sweep

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One moving security camera you can observe for one cycle

Watch its field of view and identify a blind interval.

- **Weak:** Identify its next direction of travel.
- **Standard:** Identify one blind interval and the route it exposes.
- **Strong:** Identify two such intervals if they exist.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tags:** Operator

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
