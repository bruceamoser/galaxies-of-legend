# Confirm the Signal

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally within 8 squares who can see or hear you

Give the ally 1 Boon on their next Reason or Agility check this turn to act on your declared signal.

**Duration:** As stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Command 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Command / Maneuver

**Limits:** Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** confirm-the-signal
