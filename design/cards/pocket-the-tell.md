# Pocket the Tell

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self while you have concealment

Stow one small item you hold and gain 1 Boon on your next Agility check this turn to hide or remain Hidden. The card does not grant Hidden by itself.

**Duration:** As stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Subterfuge / Maneuver

**Limits:** Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** pocket-the-tell
