# Leave a Way Out

*You enter through the gap between physical and digital security.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - new signature, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one willing ally within 4 squares, when an exit begins to close

Use an exit you personally scouted this scene. You cannot pass a sealed barrier.

- **Weak:** move yourself up to 2 squares toward that exit.
- **Standard:** move yourself and the ally up to 2 squares each toward the exit.
- **Strong:** move both up to 4 squares each toward the exit.

**Duration:** Instant; this movement does not grant an attack or bypass a hazard.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires an open path to a previously scouted exit

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Operator; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Operator

**Limits:** Do not stack multiple instances of the same benefit; each granted Boon applies once. Pay the action and any stated time.
