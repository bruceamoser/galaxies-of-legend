# Draw the Eye

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible pursuer within weapon range

Fire a ready sidearm at the pursuer to give an ally room to leave.

- **Weak:** Deal 2 weapon damage.
- **Standard:** Deal 3 weapon damage and the pursuer takes a Bane on its next attack against one ally you can see this scene.
- **Strong:** Deal 4 weapon damage and apply the same Bane.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready weapon of the stated kind.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Outlaw; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Outlaw

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
