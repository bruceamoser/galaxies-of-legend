# Safe Shutdown

*You stop the machine from injuring the person trying to repair it.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - starter coverage, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One malfunctioning non-hostile robot within reach

Isolate a fault and stop the robot safely. This cannot override a hostile controller or command an unwilling sentient being.

- **Weak:** stop it until your next turn; its fault remains.
- **Standard:** stop it until deliberately restarted.
- **Strong:** stop it until restarted and give the next repair of that fault a Boon.

**Duration:** As stated; the repair Boon expires at the end of the scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires access to a service panel and a toolkit

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Robotics
