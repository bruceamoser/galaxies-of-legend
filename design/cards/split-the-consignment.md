# Split the Consignment

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One consignment under your control and two compatible carriers

Allocate labeled packages and produce a manifest for each carrier. The transfer still requires ordinary handling time.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Trade 3

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Trade / Maneuver

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
