# Keep It Running

*You coax one more shift out of a system that has already given up.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One vehicle or ship system within reach; 10 minutes

Make a temporary repair to a damaged system.

- **Weak:** restore 1 step to the system's die; it fails again at the end of the scene.
- **Standard:** restore 1 step for the scene.
- **Strong:** restore 2 steps for the scene, or 1 step until properly repaired.

**Duration:** One scene, or until properly repaired on a Strong result.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a toolkit or suitable parts

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Engineering 1

**Type:** Skill / Technical

**Tags:** Support / Technical

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
