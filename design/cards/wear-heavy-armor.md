# Wear Heavy Armour

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self

You can buy and use [the matching armour](../equipment/heavy-armor.md). While wearing it, apply its printed protection and limitations.

**Duration:** While wearing the armour.

**Equipment / acquisition:** The armour is purchased separately. This card grants none.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Armor 2

**Type:** Training / Passive

**Tags:** Armor / Passive

**Limits:** Use the normal resistance rules. This card supplies no additional resistance.
