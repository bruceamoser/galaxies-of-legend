# Aid

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing ally

Help with a declared task you can plausibly assist. Give the ally 1 Boon on their next attribute roll for that task before the start of your next turn. Outside combat, contribute Aid or your own roll to the same group task.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Tags:** Maneuver

**Limits:** Spend the listed action and observe any stated limit.
