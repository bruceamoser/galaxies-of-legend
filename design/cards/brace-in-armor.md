# Brace in Armor

*You brace where the next impact will land.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - starter coverage, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when taking Kinetic damage

Brace against one impact while wearing armour. Apply the reduction after resistance; the core minimum damage still applies.

- **Weak:** reduce the damage by 1.
- **Standard:** reduce the damage by 2.
- **Strong:** reduce the damage by 3.

**Duration:** One attack.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires worn armour; grants no armour

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Armor
