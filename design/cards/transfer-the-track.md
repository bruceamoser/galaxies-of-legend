# Transfer the Track

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing gunner at another working ship weapon station; same ship and tracked target

Send the target bearing to their display. This supplies a bearing only and does not grant a target lock or an attack.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 3

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Gunnery / Maneuver

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** transfer-the-track
