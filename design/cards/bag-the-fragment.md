# Bag the Fragment

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible scene or item

Collect and label one reachable sample. Gain 1 Boon on your next Reason check this scene to investigate its source.

**Duration:** As stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Investigation 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Investigation / Maneuver

**Limits:** Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
