# Compare Impressions

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Two accessible impressions you can inspect side by side

Determine whether their visible shape and dimensions match. A match does not identify who made them.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Investigation 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Investigation / Maneuver

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
