# The Right Story

*You make the room expect the person you are pretending to be.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - new signature, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when an NPC challenges your cover with one piece of evidence

Offer an explanation consistent with facts already established. You cannot erase evidence or force belief.

- **Weak:** gain a Boon on your next check to answer that challenge.
- **Standard:** gain a Boon on that check and one follow-up in the same conversation.
- **Strong:** gain those Boons and learn what verification the NPC will attempt next.

**Duration:** Until the conversation ends or contradictory evidence is produced.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a still-plausible cover identity; once per conversation

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Con Artist; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Con Artist

**Limits:** Do not stack multiple instances of the same benefit; each granted Boon applies once. Pay the action and any stated time.
