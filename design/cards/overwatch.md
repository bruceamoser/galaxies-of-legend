# Overwatch

*You cover the approach and wait for someone to make a mistake.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature that moves within 8 squares

When a visible creature moves within range, make a ranged attack against it.

- **Weak:** 2 Kinetic damage.
- **Standard:** 3 Kinetic damage.
- **Strong:** 5 Kinetic damage; the target's movement ends.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 1

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Tags:** Attack / Reaction

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** overwatch
