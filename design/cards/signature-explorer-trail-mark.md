# Trail Mark

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One junction you can reach

Mark a route in a way your crew can recognize.

- **Weak:** Leave a mark visible from one approach.
- **Standard:** Leave marks legible from every approach.
- **Strong:** Also indicate the nearest safe retreat you know.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Explorer; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Explorer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
