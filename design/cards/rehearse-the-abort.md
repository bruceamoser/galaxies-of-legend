# Recover the Approach

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility
**Target / range / shape:** One personal-scale craft you are piloting and an accessible route clear of a hazard

Make progress toward aborting a dangerous approach. At 3 progress reach the declared holding point or exit if the craft can follow that route. Use the Starship Manual for ship-scale actions.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

**Duration:** Progress remains on the declared task until it resolves or circumstances invalidate it.


**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires appropriate access and any equipment named in the effect.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Piloting 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Piloting / Main Action

**Limits:** Spend the listed action. Resolve the printed result once.
