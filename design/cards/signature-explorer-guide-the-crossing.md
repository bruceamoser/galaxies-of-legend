# Guide the Crossing

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally within speaking distance before they check to cross terrain you can observe

Give the ally 1 Boon on their Agility or Fortitude check to cross the obstacle. Name the route or timing you are calling out before the roll.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Explorer; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Explorer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
