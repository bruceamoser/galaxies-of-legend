# Steady Hands

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self before a task that requires precise handling

Gain 1 Boon on your next Agility check this turn to handle, place or retrieve an object. This does not apply to attacks.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Training / Utility

**Tags:** Maneuver

**Limits:** Spend the listed action and observe any stated limit.
