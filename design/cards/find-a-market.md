# Find a Market

*You find the buyer who actually has room for what you carry.*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - starter coverage, untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One settlement or station market; one hour

Find a legitimate buyer or seller for one stated ordinary cargo or supply. The SA sets progress, usually 3. Local scarcity and prices still apply.

- **Weak:** 1 progress; pay a locally quoted listing fee or attract an unwanted inquiry.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; identify one available counterparty.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; identify two counterparties and their quoted terms.

**Duration:** Until local market conditions change.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires access to local trade information

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Trade 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Trade

**Limits:** One instance of this card's benefit per target or task; repeat only for new progress, a new trigger or a changed situation. Pay the printed action and time.
