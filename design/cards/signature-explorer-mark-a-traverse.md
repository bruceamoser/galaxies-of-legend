# Mark a Traverse

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One route you have traversed; one hour

Make a route record showing known hazards and shelter.

- **Weak:** Record the waypoints without timing estimates.
- **Standard:** Record the route with travel times and safe stops.
- **Strong:** Also record the last safe point to turn back before each known hazard.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Explorer; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Explorer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
