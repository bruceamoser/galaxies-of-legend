# Shield the Withdrawal

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing ally before a visible attack against them is rolled; you have a ready shield

If you can interpose your shield, give the ally 1 Boon to defend, or impose 1 Bane if the attacker is the one rolling. After the attack resolves the ally may move 1 square toward available cover if still able. The ally remains the target.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready shield.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Soldier

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.

**ID:** signature-soldier-shield-the-withdrawal
