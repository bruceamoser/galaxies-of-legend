# Bench Test

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One detached electrical or mechanical component within reach

Test the component on a safe power supply before fitting it.

- **Weak:** Determine whether it works under light load.
- **Standard:** Determine its operating limits and whether it fits the proposed installation.
- **Strong:** As Standard. Identify a compatible substitute among parts you can inspect.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** Toolkit and a safe test supply

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Technician; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Technician

**Limits:** Pay the action and stated time. This card grants no improvement to installed system dice.
