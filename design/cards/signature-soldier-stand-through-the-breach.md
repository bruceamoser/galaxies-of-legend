# Stand Through the Breach

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** Yourself when a visible attack would push you from a doorway you occupy

Brace against the push after damage resolves.

- **Weak:** Reduce the push by one square.
- **Standard:** Reduce it by two squares.
- **Strong:** Reduce it by three squares.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Soldier

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
