# Miracle Worker

*You rebuild the failing system from memory, scrap and stubbornness.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One ship or vehicle you can reach

Perform an impossible repair.

- **Weak:** restore one system to d6.
- **Standard:** restore two systems to d8.
- **Strong:** restore all systems to at least d8 and remove one system wound.

**Duration:** Permanent until damaged again.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a toolkit and spare parts

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Engineering 3

**Type:** Skill / Technical

**Tags:** Technical / Ship

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** miracle-worker
