# Change of Wardrobe

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** Yourself, out of direct view, with a change of clothing

Abandon an identifiable cover while retaining what you carry.

- **Weak:** Change one identifying feature.
- **Standard:** Change your visible outfit and apparent occupation.
- **Strong:** Also identify a plausible exit for someone in the new role.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Con Artist; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Con Artist

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
