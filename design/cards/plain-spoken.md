# Plain Spoken

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self when asking for cooperation in a shared language

Once per scene, gain 1 Boon on a Guile check to make a direct request if you state your purpose truthfully and offer a concrete reason to help.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Training / Utility

**Tags:** Passive

**Limits:** Spend the listed action and observe any stated limit.
