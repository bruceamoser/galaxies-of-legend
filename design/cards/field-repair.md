# Field Repair

*You put the machine back together well enough to finish the job.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One damaged device or system within reach

Repair a device or system.

- **Weak:** restore 1 HP or 1 system step; the repair is fragile and fails on a later 1.
- **Standard:** restore 2 HP or 1 system step.
- **Strong:** restore 3 HP or 2 system steps.

**Duration:** Permanent until damaged again.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a toolkit

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Engineering 1

**Type:** Skill / Technical

**Tags:** Technical / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** field-repair
