# Read a Custom

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One observed practice, record, or willing interlocutor

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.
