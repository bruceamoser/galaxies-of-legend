# Find the Shared Interest

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing counterpart or group that can communicate with you

Advance a negotiation toward a concrete request. At 3 progress learn the terms under which the counterpart would consider the request, or the specific reason no terms are available. They retain the choice to agree.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

**Duration:** Progress remains on the declared task until it resolves or circumstances invalidate it.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires appropriate access and any equipment named in the effect.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Influence 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Influence / Main Action

**Limits:** Spend the listed action. Resolve the printed result once.
