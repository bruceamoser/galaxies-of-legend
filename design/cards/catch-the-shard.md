# Catch the Shard

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent ally before their defense against visible flying debris; ready shield

Give the ally a Boon on that defense if you can interpose the shield.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Armor 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Armor / Reaction

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** catch-the-shard
