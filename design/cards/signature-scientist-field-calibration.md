# Field Calibration

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One working measuring instrument within reach and a known reference

Check the instrument against the reference.

- **Weak:** Identify whether its reading drifts.
- **Standard:** Correct the reading for one measurement.
- **Strong:** Correct it for the scene under the same conditions.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.
