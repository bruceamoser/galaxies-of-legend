# Pocket Story

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One listener who asks about a minor contradiction in your cover

Offer a mundane explanation consistent with what they already know.

- **Weak:** They repeat the question in front of a witness.
- **Standard:** They defer checking the detail until the conversation ends.
- **Strong:** They offer a detail you can use to make your story consistent.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Con Artist; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Con Artist

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
