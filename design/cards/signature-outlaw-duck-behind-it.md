# Duck Behind It

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Yourself when targeted by a visible ranged attack and adjacent to usable cover

Use the adjacent obstruction to shelter. Resolve this before your defense roll.

- **Weak:** Turn toward the attacker without moving.
- **Standard:** Gain a Boon on this defense roll.
- **Strong:** Gain the Boon and move one square into available cover after the attack resolves.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Outlaw; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Outlaw

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
