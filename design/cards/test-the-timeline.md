# Test the Timeline

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible timeline and one independently timed event

Place the event on the timeline and identify a direct contradiction if the recorded times rule out the claimed order.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.
