# Call the Vote

*You put the question plainly enough that the room has to answer it.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One group that can hear and understand you

Force a decision or gather support. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; one wavering voice joins you.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; the group moves toward your position.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; the group commits and one ally gains a Boon on their next social check.

**Duration:** The decision persists.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Social / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
