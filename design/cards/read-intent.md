# Read Intent

*You watch the hands, the pause and the answer that arrived too quickly.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature you can hear and see

Study the target's manner.

- **Weak:** 1 progress; learn one surface emotion.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; learn what the target wants from this conversation.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; also learn one thing the target is afraid you will ask.

**Duration:** The information persists.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Influence 1

**Type:** Skill / Social

**Tags:** Social / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** read-intent
