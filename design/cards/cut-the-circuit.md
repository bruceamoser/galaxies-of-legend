# Cut the Circuit

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Your working ship weapon station when a weapon fault occurs before the shot

Safe that weapon and cancel the shot. The station action is spent. Further use requires restoring normal operation.

**Duration:** Instant unless the effect states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 3

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Gunnery / Reaction

**Limits:** Pay the listed action each use. Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** cut-the-circuit
