# Recover the Recorder

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible damaged recorder; one uninterrupted hour

Read a damaged recording medium without returning the recorder to service. Erased or physically missing material is unavailable.

- **Weak:** Recover a fragment from one time window you specify.
- **Standard:** Recover the intact material from that window and identify gaps.
- **Strong:** As Standard. Recover intact material from a second window.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** Toolkit and a compatible reader

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Technician; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Technician

**Limits:** Pay the action and stated time. This card grants no improvement to installed system dice.
