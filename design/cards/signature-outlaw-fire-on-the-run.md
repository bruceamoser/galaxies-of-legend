# Fire on the Run

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within weapon range after moving this turn

Make a ranged attack with a ready sidearm while moving between positions.

- **Weak:** Deal 3 weapon damage.
- **Standard:** Deal 4 weapon damage and move one square.
- **Strong:** Deal 5 weapon damage and move up to two squares.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready weapon of the stated kind.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Outlaw; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Outlaw

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
