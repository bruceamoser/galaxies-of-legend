# Check the Gripper

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One crew-controlled robot you can access

Check its available manipulators and gain 1 Boon on your next Knowledge check this scene to configure it or diagnose a handling problem. This grants no extra command.

**Duration:** As stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Robotics 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Robotics / Maneuver

**Limits:** Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
