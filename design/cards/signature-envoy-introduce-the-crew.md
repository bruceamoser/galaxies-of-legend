# Introduce the Crew

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally and a contact within speaking distance

State the crew’s shared purpose. Give the ally 1 Boon on their next Guile or Reason roll this scene to negotiate with the contact.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Envoy

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
