# Break Pursuit

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Yourself with a usable route out of sight

Move away along terrain you can see, paying normal terrain costs.

- **Weak:** Move one square.
- **Standard:** Move up to two squares.
- **Strong:** Move up to three squares.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.
