# Drift Pilot

*You let the ship slide and catch the drift where the manoeuvring thrusters cannot.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one ship you are piloting

Ignore difficult terrain or one environmental penalty until the end of your next turn. On a Strong result, also gain a Boon on your next Piloting check.

**Duration:** End of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ship or vehicle you are piloting

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Spacer; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Movement / Ship

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-spacer-drift-pilot
