# Clear a Treatment Space

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One reachable work surface and its immediate surroundings

Prepare a clean place to examine one patient. This restores no HP.

- **Weak:** Identify the contamination or obstruction that needs removing.
- **Standard:** Prepare the surface if ordinary cleaning supplies are available.
- **Strong:** Also set aside a clean place for instruments and used dressings.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Medic; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Medic

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
