# Read the Room

*You find the person who actually decides and the person everyone is watching.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One social gathering or negotiation

Read the balance of the room. The SA sets the progress needed (usually 3).

- **Weak:** 1 progress; identify the most influential person present.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; identify what they want and who opposes them.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; also learn one concession they would accept.

**Duration:** The information persists for the scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Social / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
