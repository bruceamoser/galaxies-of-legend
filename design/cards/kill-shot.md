# Kill Shot

*You take the shot you have been waiting for since the firing started.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 12 squares

Make a precise ranged attack.

- **Weak:** 8 Kinetic damage.
- **Standard:** 12 Kinetic damage.
- **Strong:** 18 Kinetic damage; ignore the target's DR.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Tags:** Attack

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
