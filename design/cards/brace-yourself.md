# Brace Yourself

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self before a check to resist being moved or losing your footing

Gain 1 Boon on the roll if you can brace against a surface or hold. This does not create a roll against an effect that normally has none.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Training / Utility

**Tags:** Reaction

**Limits:** Spend the listed action and observe any stated limit.
