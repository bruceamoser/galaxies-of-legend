# Shoot and Slip

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within weapon range

Make a ranged attack with a ready sidearm. After resolving it, you may move one square by normal movement rules.

- **Weak:** Deal 1 weapon damage.
- **Standard:** Deal 2 weapon damage.
- **Strong:** Deal 3 weapon damage.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready weapon of the stated kind.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Outlaw; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Outlaw

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.

**ID:** signature-outlaw-shoot-and-slip
