# Hold the Angle

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within range of your ready weapon while you occupy cover

Make an attack from a fixed position.

- **Weak:** Deal 2 weapon damage.
- **Standard:** Deal 3 weapon damage and remain braced against the next forced movement this turn, gaining a Boon if a check is called for.
- **Strong:** Deal 4 weapon damage and retain that benefit until your next turn.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready weapon of the stated kind.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Soldier; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Soldier

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
