# Quote the Unit

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self before a Reason or Guile check to evaluate or negotiate a deal

Gain 1 Boon by restating the quantity, terms and total price before you respond.

**Duration:** As stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Trade 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Trade / Reaction

**Limits:** Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.
