# Field Triage

*You put your hands where they will do the most good before you know the whole story.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One wounded creature within reach

Stabilise a wounded or dying creature.

- **Weak:** stop the creature from dying; it remains at 0 HP and unconscious.
- **Standard:** stop the dying condition and restore 1 HP.
- **Strong:** restore 2 HP and remove Bleeding 1.

**Duration:** Instant; the creature remains stable unless wounded again.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a medkit

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 1

**Type:** Skill / Recovery

**Tags:** Recovery / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** field-triage
