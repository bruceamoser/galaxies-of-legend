# Borrowed Livery

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One observer within speaking distance who expects routine staff

Present a plausible role supported by your clothing or credentials.

- **Weak:** They ask for a supervisor.
- **Standard:** They let you finish one routine task under observation.
- **Strong:** They also tell you where to report next.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.
