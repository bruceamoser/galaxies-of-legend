# Basic Ranged

*You take the shot the situation allows.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 8 squares

Make a ranged attack. Use the damage type of the weapon you fire.

- **Weak:** 2 damage.
- **Standard:** 3 damage.
- **Strong:** 5 damage.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Tags:** Attack

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** basic-ranged
