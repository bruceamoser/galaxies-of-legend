# Reverse Grip

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self with a ready melee weapon and a free hand

Ready a second carried item and gain 1 Boon on your next Brawn melee attack before the end of this turn.

**Duration:** As stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires any equipment named above. Grants no equipment.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Arms 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Arms / Maneuver

**Limits:** Any granted Boon applies once and does not stack with itself.

**ID:** reverse-grip
