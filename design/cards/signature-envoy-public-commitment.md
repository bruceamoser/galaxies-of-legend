# Public Commitment

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing spokesperson and their audience within speaking distance

Give the spokesperson a chance to state one verifiable commitment.

- **Weak:** Learn which part they will defend publicly.
- **Standard:** They state the commitment and how observers can verify it.
- **Strong:** They also give a deadline and name who is responsible.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Envoy

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
