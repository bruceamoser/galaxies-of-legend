# Common Terms

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Two willing speakers within conversation range

Restate a disagreement in terms both parties understand.

- **Weak:** Identify one point of confusion.
- **Standard:** Each party confirms one condition the other needs.
- **Strong:** They also confirm one condition they can drop.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Envoy

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
