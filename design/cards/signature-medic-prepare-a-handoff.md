# Prepare a Handoff

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One patient within reach whom you can examine

Record visible conditions and immediate care already given. This does not treat the patient.

- **Weak:** Record the most urgent visible concern.
- **Standard:** Record the concerns and give the next clinician a Boon on a check to assess this patient this scene.
- **Strong:** As Standard. Record what gear has already been used on the patient.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Medic; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Medic

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
