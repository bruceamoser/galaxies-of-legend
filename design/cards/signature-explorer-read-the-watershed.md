# Read the Watershed

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One drainage basin you can observe; one hour

Read terrain and water marks before choosing a camp.

- **Weak:** Identify the direction of drainage.
- **Standard:** Identify a likely flood route and a camp outside it if available.
- **Strong:** Also identify a second camp accessible from a different approach.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Explorer

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
