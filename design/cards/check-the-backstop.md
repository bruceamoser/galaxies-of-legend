# Suppress an Approach

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility
**Target / range / shape:** One visible target in range of your ready ranged weapon

Fire to force a target back from its current position. Use the weapon’s damage type.

- **Weak:** 2 damage.
- **Standard:** 3 damage and apply Slowed 1.
- **Strong:** 4 damage and apply Slowed 2.

**Duration:** Instant.


**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ready ranged weapon

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Gunnery 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Tags:** Gunnery / Main Action

**Limits:** Spend the listed action. Resolve the printed result once.

**ID:** check-the-backstop
