# Terms of Passage

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One official who can grant supervised access; ten minutes

Offer a concrete safeguard in exchange for limited passage.

- **Weak:** Learn the minimum safeguard they would accept.
- **Standard:** Obtain supervised passage for the crew if you meet it.
- **Strong:** Also secure permission to return along the same route this scene.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Envoy; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Envoy

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
