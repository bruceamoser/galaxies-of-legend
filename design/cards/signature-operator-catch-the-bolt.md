# Catch the Bolt

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One door within reach about to close under its own mechanism

Catch the latch without defeating an active lock.

- **Weak:** Keep it open long enough to withdraw your hand.
- **Standard:** Hold it ajar until the end of your next turn.
- **Strong:** Hold it ajar that long and let one adjacent ally pass through.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Operator; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Operator

**Limits:** Each granted Boon or Bane applies once. Repeated uses do not stack.
