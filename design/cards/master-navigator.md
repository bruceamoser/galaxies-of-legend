# Master Navigator

*You hold the whole route in your head and bring the ship out where you meant to.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One ship making an FTL jump

Plot a jump with precision.

- **Weak:** arrive off target with a complication.
- **Standard:** arrive at the intended system and approach.
- **Strong:** arrive at a favourable position of your choice, with no complication.

**Duration:** One jump.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires charts or a nav computer

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Navigation 3

**Type:** Skill / Navigation

**Tags:** Navigation / Ship

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** master-navigator
