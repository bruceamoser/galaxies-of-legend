# Toxic Exposure Interview

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One conscious patient or witness within speaking distance; ten minutes

Reconstruct the route and timing of a suspected exposure. This does not remove conditions.

- **Weak:** Identify a likely exposure window.
- **Standard:** Identify a likely route and window of exposure.
- **Strong:** Also identify another person or area that may need screening.

**Duration:** Instant unless an outcome states a duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires the access, tools or equipment stated above.
