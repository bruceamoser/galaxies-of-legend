# Through the Storm

*You take the ship through the thing everyone else is steering around.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One ship and one hazard or pursuit

Navigate a hazard or escape a pursuit.

- **Weak:** get through with a system wound.
- **Standard:** get through with no system wound.
- **Strong:** get through and arrive in a favourable position.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ship you are piloting

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Spacer; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Ship / Navigation

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** signature-spacer-through-the-storm
