# Rail Rifle

*A long, heavy rifle that turns cover into a suggestion.*

## Rules

**Category:** Weapon · **Damage type:** Kinetic · **Bulk:** 2 · **Hands:** 2 · **Cost:** 180 coins

Ranged weapon. Range 12 squares. Uses the Basic Ranged action; a successful hit deals Kinetic damage. Attacks against targets behind cover ignore 1 point of cover DR.
