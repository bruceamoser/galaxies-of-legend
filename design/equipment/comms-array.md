**Status:** Playtest proposal - adapted from the OpenBaD equipment draft, untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Tool · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 1 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 60 coins

With line of sight, transmit voice or text between a ship and the surface at up to 10 km. Solid obstructions and active jamming can block the link.
