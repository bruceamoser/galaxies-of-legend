# Thermal Shelter

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Tool · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 2 · **Hands:** 2 · **Cost:** 65 coins

Deploy in ten minutes on ground that can take its anchors. Shelters two explorers from ordinary wind and precipitation. A pressure leak, flood or direct heat source can still make the site unsafe.
