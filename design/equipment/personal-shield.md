# Personal Shield

**Status:** Playtest proposal - adapted from the OpenBaD equipment draft, untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Protection · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 1 · **Hands:** 0 · **Cost:** 400 coins

Activate as a Maneuver. Grants Fire, Cold and Lightning Resistance 2 for one scene. Replace the spent cell before reactivating. A replacement costs 20 coins and takes ten minutes to fit.
