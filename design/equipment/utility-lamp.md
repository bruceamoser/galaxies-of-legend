# Utility Lamp

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Tool · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 0 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 15 coins

Illuminate a 4-square cone from the lamp. Clip it to clothing or a fixture to leave both hands free. Smoke and opaque barriers still obscure what the light reaches.
