# Climbing Rig

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Tool · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 1 · **Hands:** 2 · **Cost:** 55 coins

Includes a harness, a 20-metre line and anchors for sound surfaces. An attached line arrests a fall when it goes taut if the anchor holds. The explorer still has to reach solid footing or be hauled up.
