# Heavy Armour

**Status:** Playtest proposal - adapted from the OpenBaD equipment draft, untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Protection · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 2 · **Hands:** 0 · **Cost:** 320 coins

Grants Physical Resistance 2 and Fire Resistance 2. Reduce Speed by 1 while worn. Donning or removing it takes ten minutes.

**Required ability:** [Wear Heavy Armour](../cards/wear-heavy-armor.md). Learn it before buying or using this armour.
