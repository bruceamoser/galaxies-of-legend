# Shield Cell

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Supply · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 0 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 20 coins

Restores one activation to a personal shield. Fitting a fresh cell takes ten minutes. A spent cell can be returned to a shop that services the shield.
