# Low-Light Optics

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Sensor · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 0 · **Hands:** 0 · **Cost:** 65 coins

Reduce sight-based Banes from dim light by 1 while wearing the optics. Use only the larger reduction if a trait also reduces dim-light Banes. They provide no image in total darkness or through smoke.
