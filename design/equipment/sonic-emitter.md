# Sonic Emitter

**Status:** Playtest proposal - adapted from the OpenBaD equipment draft, untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Weapon · **Damage type:** Physical · **Bulk:** 2 · **Hands:** 2 · **Cost:** 190 coins

Ranged weapon. Range 8 squares in atmosphere. Uses Basic Ranged and deals Physical damage. On a Strong result the target also gains Deafened 1. It requires a continuous atmosphere between the weapon and the target.
