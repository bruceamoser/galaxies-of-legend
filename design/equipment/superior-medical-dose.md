# Superior Medical Dose

*A sealed medical supply for emergency HP recovery.*

## Rules

**Category:** Supply · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 1 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 120 coins

Inventory d4. Use [Administer a Medical Dose](../cards/administer-medical-dose.md): restore 16 HP before Life resistance, capped by missing HP; minimum 1 actual healing where HP is missing. No outcome roll or healer HP cost. Check inventory after use; the final use still works. This grants no wound treatment, Grit, resurrection or reversal of Last Words.

The giver spends a Maneuver and needs a free hand. Another recipient spends a Reaction unless at 0 HP. Prices retain HOL's 20/50/120 proposal for this sci-fi equivalent; local availability remains an SA decision.
