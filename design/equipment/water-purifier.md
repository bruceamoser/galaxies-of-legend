# Water Purifier

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Tool · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 1 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 35 coins

Treats one explorer’s daily drinking water in ten minutes. Removes sediment and ordinary biological contamination from fresh water. Dissolved toxins, salt and radioactive contamination require specialized treatment.
