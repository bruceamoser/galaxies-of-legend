# Drone Controller

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Communications · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 1 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 65 coins

Pair the controller to one compatible personal drone before the scene. It sends orders and displays reports during the paid command described in [Drones, sprites and companions](../rules/companions.md). It grants no drone, extra action or independent watch. A blocked signal interrupts the command.
