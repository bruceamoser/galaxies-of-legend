# Spare Air Cylinder

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Supply · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 1 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 25 coins

A charged cylinder replaces the spent air supply of a compatible vac suit or rebreather. Fit it in breathable air or use the equipment’s isolated changeover port. It holds the same amount of air as the supply it replaces.

**ID:** air-cylinder
