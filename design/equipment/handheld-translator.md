# Handheld Translator

**Status:** Playtest proposal - untested.

## Rules

**Category:** Communications · **Damage type:** None · **Bulk:** 0 · **Hands:** 1 · **Cost:** 60 coins

Comes with Galactic Standard and one ordinary language library chosen when acquired. Its microphone handles ordinary speech and its camera can read compatible writing or capture visible signing supported by the library. It presents written or spoken output, or a visible sign demonstration where supported. Knell Pattern pulses require a compatible contact-pad adapter. The SA establishes what libraries and adapters are available.

It translates routine wording while it can capture the message. It cannot decrypt a file, establish intent or supply a library it lacks. Use [Languages and translation](../rules/languages.md) when communication is uncertain.
