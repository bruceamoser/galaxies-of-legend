# Pellori

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Reedglass

Pellori tend some of Reedglass’s oldest living settlements. Their growers and researchers disagree over how much a community should change to make room for useful new life.

## Appearance

Each Pellori is an individual with one nervous system. Branching tissues rise above flexible supports, with two articulated tendrils for handling tools. Exposed patches can change texture around some biological cultures. An experienced grower knows which changes are familiar and when to ask for a sample.

## Home and history

Reedglass is a wetland world in Osmere. Its settlements built separate grow beds to avoid losing an entire food supply to one flood or disease. Water moves between them in channels that also carry seeds and spores. A decision about what to plant can become a decision about what every neighbor will have to live with.

## Society and relations

Some basin councils require a season of isolated trials before a new crop enters the channels. Others trade seedlings early and argue that wider variety can save a settlement after a bad harvest. Nacreth gate crews and Pellori growers often cooperate on water projects, though they may disagree over when a channel is safe to reopen.

## Seed exchange

Families in some Reedglass towns bring a sample from their own grow bed to a seasonal exchange. Hosts keep a separate tray for each sample and ask who else has handled it. A grower who refuses to name a source may still be welcomed to the meal, while their sample waits outside the channels.

## Explorers

Research Enclave expedition guides bring samples home from places the basin councils have never seen. Service Cooperative field medics learn to care for people who cannot reach a permanent clinic. A Scientist might leave to trace the source of a crop blight. A Medic might be looking for a treatment that their home settlement cannot provide.

**Your explorer.** What living thing did someone trust you to carry? Which rule would you break to keep it alive?

**At the table.** A Pellori with Close the Break takes Bleeding 2. On their next turn they spend a Maneuver to bring it to Bleeding 1, then use their Main Action to deal with the danger that caused it.

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Close the Break.** *Living fibers draw together under your grasp.* Once per scene, spend a Maneuver to reduce your temporary Bleeding rating by 1, to a minimum of 0.
2. **Taste of a Habitat.** *You have learned what belongs in a grow bed and what arrived yesterday.* Gain 1 Boon on checks to identify unfamiliar biological growth or trace contamination through a living system using a sample safe to handle.
3. **Living Graft.** *You place the tissue where it has the best chance to take.* Once per day, add 1 progress after you get Standard or Strong on a wound treatment roll for a wound with a Physical wound-table result. Use the normal capability, time, equipment and daily attempt limit.
4. **Slow the Spread.** *You isolate the exposed growth before it takes hold.* Once per scene, when you would gain temporary Poisoned, spend your Reaction to reduce the incoming rating by 1, to a minimum of 0.
5. **Root Hold.** *Your lower supports settle into ground that gives way under a boot.* Soft, waterlogged earth does not increase your voluntary movement cost. It still needs to support your weight.
