# Weldkin

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Kiln Nine

Weldkin descend from living bodies grown for foundry work. Their settlements remember when a contract was something written about them and when they first had the power to negotiate one themselves.

## Appearance

Woven ceramic tendons show beneath dark living skin, particularly around the forked leg joints and narrow working arms. The body was designed for controlled force near industrial machinery. A Weldkin needs food, rest and medical care like other living explorers.

## Home and history

Kiln Nine lies in the Ferric belt. When the foundry lost its last owner, its workers retained the machinery and the inventory records that listed their founders. Some families still visit the old production line to learn which entries refer to people they knew.

## Society and relations

Cooperative shops commonly insist on a clear right to leave a job. Some keep a public copy of every labor agreement. Others prefer private contracts because the old inventory system taught them what powerful owners can do with complete records. Human freight companies that expect a crew to sign before seeing the terms can meet a firm refusal.

## Read the terms aloud

Some Kiln Nine shops begin a contract by reading the terms in front of the people who will do the work. Anyone can ask where the tools go if the job ends early. Other shops keep agreements private and use independent witnesses. Both customs grew from arguments about which records workers could demand to see.

## Explorers

Machine Commune attendants work with automated equipment that has its own maintenance limits. Service Cooperative mechanics share skills across shops. A Technician may travel to recover tools made by the founders. A Spacer may sign on precisely because they want a life away from the foundry.

**Your explorer.** Who offered you a job you were free to refuse? What would make you leave a crew now?

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Controlled Force.** *You can feel the exact point where a stuck part begins to move.* Once per scene, after a Standard or Strong check on a progress task to free a jammed mechanism with suitable tools, add 1 progress.
2. **Ceramic Weave.** *The tendons that once worked beside furnaces still carry the strain.* You have Fire resistance 1.
3. **Built for the Long Shift.** *Your frame was made to keep its alignment under load.* Increase maximum HP by 2.
4. **Let Go Clean.** *You know what to release before the rest of the load follows.* Once per scene, when you gain Grappled 1 from a machine, restraint or moving load that you can physically disengage from, spend your Reaction to remove it. This does not apply to higher ratings.
5. **Leave It Safe.** *When you hand off the job, you show the next person where to put their hands.* Once per scene after your repair restores a ship system, give 1 Boon to the next ally’s check using that system before the scene ends if you can brief them. This does not change the system die or its load roll.
