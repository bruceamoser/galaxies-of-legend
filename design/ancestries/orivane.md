# Orivane

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Lattice Moons

Orivane are closely associated with the Lattice Moons, where a safe route can change when someone moves a cargo module. Many spend their working lives making crossings possible for the people who follow them.

## Appearance

Orivane have long forelimbs, hinged legs and wide grasping feet. Paired balance sacs flank a flattened jaw. A rail or tether gives the body several ways to brace itself while a station turns around it.

## Home and history

The Lattice Moons are a group of habitats in Arlet built around small rubble moons. When permanent corridors proved expensive to keep aligned, early builders made temporary connections and posted crossing instructions at both ends. A shortcut could outlast the station that first paid for it.

## Society and relations

Many crews leave their names on the crossings they inspect. Some treat the name as a promise to answer questions about the route. Others want inspection records kept by a public office, since a familiar name can outlive the last reliable check. Offworld crews tend to welcome an Orivane rigger and then discover how many questions they have about a route everyone else calls safe.

## Leave a route note

An Orivane crossing marker is often small enough to fit in one hand. It records the last safe handhold and where to stop if the next section starts moving. Some travelers add a personal note about the place on the far side. An old marker can become the only invitation a stranger has to find a settlement.

## Explorers

Nomad Fleet scouts carry news between stations that rarely see the same traffic. Free Station riggers keep the transfer corridors usable. A Spacer may take shipboard work to get beyond a route they know by heart. An Explorer might follow a connection drawn on an old chart that ends where no current dock is listed.

**Your explorer.** Whose name was on the first dangerous crossing you made? What did you find when you looked for them?

**At the table.** An Orivane with Long Reach opens a control latch two squares away while keeping a firm grip on the rail. They still use the action the latch would normally take.

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Long Reach.** *Your forelimb reaches the latch before you need to move.* Interact with a small object up to 2 squares away through a clear path, using the normal action and a free hand. Attacks, healing and other abilities keep their printed range.
2. **Tether Runner.** *You pull yourself along the line without fighting its swing.* Moving along a fixed line in low or zero gravity costs you 1 movement per square. You need a free hand and a reachable line.
3. **Catch and Turn.** *You catch the rail and use the swing to put your feet down.* Once per scene, when forced movement ends with a fixed handhold within reach and you have a free hand, spend your Reaction to move 1 square to an accessible space adjoining it. Resolve any collision before using this trait.
4. **Know Which Way.** *Your balance organs catch the turn before your eyes do.* Gain 1 Boon on checks to orient yourself or align a load when local gravity or a rotating structure changes your frame of reference.
5. **Quick to the Rail.** *You are already moving toward the next hold.* Increase your base Speed by 1.
