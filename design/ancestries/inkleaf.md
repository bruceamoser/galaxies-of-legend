# Inkleaf

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Tern Orchard

Inkleaf were uplifted from canopy browsers. The stations that once housed their training gardens now belong to communities that argue over what the uplift was for and who should tell its story.

## Appearance

Four climbing limbs bear an Inkleaf’s weight while two forward limbs handle tools. The broad pads can grip thin branches and flexible trellises. Ordinary rooms built around waist-high worktops can be more awkward for an Inkleaf than a suspended garden.

## Home and history

Tern Orchard began as a set of growing trials in Tal Oren. Early Inkleaf families claimed abandoned trellises, then linked the growing bays with paths built from retired cargo supports. Many of the routes still cross several households’ gardens.

## Society and relations

Garden assemblies keep through routes and air lines in common. A resident may own a crop without owning the trellis it grows on. Offworld buyers often ask for exclusive access to an entire bay. Some assemblies will trade a harvest but refuse a contract that would close the path through it.

## The first crossing

When a garden route is rebuilt, children often get the first supervised crossing. The builders stay below to see where they hesitate. A path that works for an adult with two free hands might be a poor route for a child carrying food. The assembly can ask for changes before it takes responsibility for maintenance.

## Explorers

Free Station riggers take jobs where a crew needs someone to see a route above the deck. Frontier Settlement scouts look for places a new community can grow food. An Inkleaf Technician may want to build a garden with fewer rules than the one they left. An Explorer may search for the records of the original uplift.

**Your explorer.** Which shared route did you help keep open? Who wanted to close it, and what did you owe them?

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Canopy Route.** *You see a second path above the floor.* Climbing intact surfaces with adequate holds costs you 1 movement per square. Roll as normal if the climb is uncertain and consequential.
2. **Catch a Hold.** *A climbing limb hooks the first support within reach.* Once per scene, when you begin to fall past an accessible adjacent handhold, spend your Reaction to catch it and stop there. Your support must bear your weight.
3. **Between the Braces.** *You fold your climbing limbs in and slip through.* Gain 1 Boon on checks to move through a tight opening you can fit through or to free yourself with Force while Grappled.
4. **Split Grip.** *Your climbing limbs carry your weight while you work.* On a climbable surface with adequate holds, you may remain in place and use both equipment hands. You need free hands as usual when moving if the climb requires them.
5. **Overhead Shortcut.** *You swing from one support to the next.* Once per scene as a Maneuver, move up to 2 squares along reachable, intact overhead supports with a free hand. This uses your Maneuver instead of your movement allowance. Roll if the crossing is uncertain and consequential.
