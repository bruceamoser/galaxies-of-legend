# Tessari

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Kett Anchor

Tessari freight towns depend on lifts and gantries. Many communities judge a promise by who turns up when the route fails and there is still work to do.

## Appearance

Three short legs support a broad frame. An arch of segmented collarbones shelters the head and distributes loads. Two strong arms reach below the arch. A Tessari is often widest where a Human-sized suit would expect the neck and shoulders to narrow.

## Home and history

Kett Anchor is a dense rocky world whose towns grew along the walls of deep ravines. The oldest lifts were built by households that could not afford a separate route for each settlement. When a lift failed, its operators had to decide which supplies to carry by hand and which neighbors to ask for help.

## Society and relations

Many gantries still post the names of the households or shops responsible for inspections. A promise made in front of the load crew carries weight. Some Tessari think those obligations make towns worth living in. Others want a chance to choose their own work without answering for a contract made before they were born.

## The names on the lift

When a neighborhood replaces an old lift, the crew can carry its nameplate to the new platform. The plate records the people who took responsibility for the first installation. Families sometimes argue over whether adding their own names honors that work or binds the next generation to a promise it never made.

## Explorers

Planetary Clans produce squad coordinators accustomed to sharing a route with several families. Service Cooperative riggers keep heavy infrastructure working. A Soldier might protect crews moving supplies through a dangerous port. A Technician might take an offworld job to earn enough to replace a lift nobody at home can afford.

**Your explorer.** Whose promise still follows you? Would you honor it if nobody from home could find out?

**At the table.** A Tessari with Hold the Line sees an adjacent crewmate about to be pushed off a loading platform. They spend a Reaction and take up to 2 squares off the push, giving the crewmate a chance to stay aboard.

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Tripod Stance.** *Your legs lock into three solid points.* As a Maneuver while you have firm footing, set your Stability to at least 2 until the start of your next turn or until you leave your square, whichever comes first.
2. **Hold the Line.** *You take the load before it can carry your companion away.* Once per scene, when an adjacent ally within reach is pushed or pulled and you have firm footing, spend your Reaction to reduce their forced movement by up to 2 squares after their Stability is applied.
3. **Built to Carry.** *You settle a load across your frame and keep going.* Increase Capacity by 3 Bulk.
4. **Up Again.** *One leg finds purchase and brings the other two under you.* Standing from Prone costs you 1 movement instead of 2.
5. **Take the Weight.** *When someone is trapped, you know where to put your strength.* Gain 1 Boon on checks to hold a failing structure in place for someone else or to pull a creature free of debris.
