# Ambril

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Morrowline

Ambril built a chain of settlements across the narrow habitable band of Morrowline. Their shelter keepers are known for opening their doors to strangers, even when those strangers have nowhere else to go.

## Appearance

Three splayed legs carry an Ambril’s radial body. Its pleated collar opens to shed heat and folds close in cold air. People who know an Ambril well can recognize discomfort in the angle of the folds before it says a word.

## Home and history

On Morrowline in the Vey system, the same face of the planet always meets its star. The settlements grew where power from the lit side could be traded for cold storage on the dark side. Early shelters pooled their reserves after several shut their doors during a long outage and lost the crews who maintained their lines.

## Society and relations

Many shelters still keep an open-door pledge. Each household takes a watch and declares how much heat or food it can spare. Councils disagree about whether visitors owe labor after accepting help. Offworld carriers sometimes mistake the pledge for a promise of unlimited supplies.

## The door watch

Shelters along the darkward route hang a lamp outside when there is room to take someone in. A keeper taking the watch walks the short way out to the next marker at dusk to see whether anyone is still coming. Offworld Ambril sometimes keep a lamp at their berth even when the station uses illuminated door signs.

## Explorers

Frontier Settlement guides know which doors will be open after the last exchange closes. Service Cooperative medics often spend their first years riding supply runs between shelters. An Explorer may sign on to survey a new route. A Medic might leave to learn how other crews keep people alive far from help.

**Your explorer.** Did your household keep a shelter open, or were you the one who needed its light?

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Shed the Heat.** *Your collar spreads as the heat hits.* Once per scene, when you gain temporary Burning, spend your Reaction to reduce its rating by 1, to a minimum of 0.
2. **Hold the Warmth.** *You pull your limbs close and let the cold pass.* You have Cold resistance 1.
3. **The Long Crossing.** *You keep enough in reserve for the distance home.* Gain 1 Boon on checks to endure environmental heat, cold or exertion during overland travel.
4. **Shelter Keeper.** *You make sure the person beside you gets warm first.* Once per scene as a Maneuver, use appropriate shelter or insulation to reduce temporary Freezing by 1 on yourself or one adjacent creature. You must be able to reach the target.
5. **Feet on Frost.** *Three points of contact leave you room to shift your weight.* Ice and frost do not increase your voluntary movement cost. Other terrain costs and hazards apply.
