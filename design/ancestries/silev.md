# Silev

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Knell Basin

Silev are known around Khor for finding faults in structures that look sound. In Knell Basin, the question after a repair is often who has checked it under load.

## Appearance

Four stout legs carry a low, wide body. Flexible mineral plates lie over the ribs and working arms. With a forearm against a rigid surface, a Silev can feel vibrations transmitted from machinery or a load shifting nearby.

## Home and history

The rail towns of Knell Basin grew on seams that vibrate as the rock heats and cools. Routes that worked in the morning could bind later in the day. Early rail crews kept samples and records of repairs so the next shift could tell a normal change from the beginning of a failure.

## Society and relations

Some workshops post failed parts where apprentices can handle them. An inspector is expected to explain what the crew should watch for after a repair. Practices differ over who gets the final word, especially when a senior operator’s experience contradicts a new instrument. Tessari freight crews value Silev inspections but can resent a closure without a clear reopening time.

## The shelf of failures

A Knell Basin shop may keep an old fastening, a bent plate and a section of cracked rail near its entrance. New apprentices are asked what failed and how they would notice it happening again. Some pieces have several explanations attached because the people who found them never agreed.

## Explorers

Service Cooperative mechanics often leave the basin to work on ships with worn frames. Research Enclave forensic aides study what went wrong after a structure failed. A Technician may want to keep an old route in service. A Scientist may be following a vibration that no familiar machine could produce.

**Your explorer.** Which failure taught you the most? Did your teacher get the cause right?

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Set Against the Deck.** *Four stout legs settle under your weight.* Your Stability is at least 1 while you have firm footing.
2. **Plated Ribs.** *Overlapping plates take the first edge off an impact.* You have Physical resistance 1. Apply the normal rule for multiple sources of resistance.
3. **Through the Frame.** *A motor’s rhythm reaches you through its housing.* While touching a rigid surface, gain 1 Boon on checks to locate a moving mechanism or structural fault by vibration. The vibration must reach the surface you touch.
4. **Hear It Failing.** *You feel the change before the support goes.* Once per scene, when a support you are touching begins to fail, spend your Reaction to move up to 1 square to an accessible space. Resolve the hazard using your new position.
5. **Show Your Work.** *You mark the fault so the next person can get straight to it.* Once per scene, after you reach Standard or Strong on a check to diagnose a mechanical fault, give 1 Boon to the next allied check to repair that fault before the scene ends if you can explain what you found.
