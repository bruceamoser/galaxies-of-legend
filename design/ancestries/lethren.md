# Lethren

**Status:** Playtest

**Starting values:** HP +2, base Speed 6, +0 coins.

**Origin:** Gloam Arc

Lethren settlements grew around the archive shafts of Gloam Arc. They are sought out for records that other ports have lost and sometimes blamed for keeping records that other ports would rather forget.

## Appearance

A large ocular lens sits between an adult Lethren’s patterned cheek sails. Its narrow body rests on folded walking limbs. Sail markings vary by individual. Familiar companions can learn quiet signals from their movement.

## Home and history

Gloam Arc spends long intervals in the shadow of its parent planet in Neral. Its first sheltered settlements stored public records down shaded shafts, where equipment stayed cooler and surface closures could not cut off every copy. Over time the reading rooms became schools, arbitration halls and places to leave messages for returning ships.

## Society and relations

Many archives keep a public record of what was changed and who changed it. They differ about who may see the original. Some protect testimony from officials named in it. Others insist that a sealed record can conceal as much harm as it prevents. Contact ports prize a Lethren interpreter who can explain why a refusal to disclose a record is sometimes a promise kept.

## The witness copy

Some archives give a witness two copies of a statement. The archive keeps one and the witness can carry the other to a place of their choosing. A copy given to an outsider can be a sign of trust. It can also mean the witness expects the archive to come under pressure.

## Explorers

Archive Network forensic aides follow missing entries, altered dates and disputed names. Contact Port interpreters learn which gestures are meant to be private. An Operator may recover records before a buyer can destroy them. An Envoy might carry testimony between communities that refuse to meet.

**Your explorer.** Whose testimony would you protect even if keeping it cost you access to the archive?

## Rules

Choose two traits. Each trait states its benefit, action and limits. Apply the normal Boon and Bane rules and equipment requirements.

1. **Quiet Channel.** *Your sails turn just far enough for a friend to read them.* As a Maneuver, send a brief, prearranged visual instruction to a willing ally within Far who can see you. The ally needs to know the signal.
2. **Twilight Eyes.** *Faint light still gives you an edge to follow.* Ignore 1 Bane imposed by dim light on checks that rely on sight. Total darkness and other obscuring effects still apply.
3. **Lost in the Flicker.** *You move when the lamps change.* Once per scene, spend a Maneuver to become Hidden from view behind cover or concealment that can hide your whole body, provided no observer can see you when you do it. Use the normal rules for ending Hidden.
4. **A Detail Out of Place.** *You remember how the room looked before.* Once per scene, gain 1 Boon on a check to find a physical change in a place you personally examined earlier.
5. **Eyes on the Exit.** *You know where the light falls before trouble starts.* Once per scene, when an enemy attack targets you in dim light and you can see the threat, spend your Reaction before the defence roll to gain 1 Boon on it.
