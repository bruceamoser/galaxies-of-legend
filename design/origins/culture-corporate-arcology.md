# Corporate Arcology

*You grew up inside a company that had its own schools, clinics and security service.*

*Who did you learn to smile at, and who never learned your name?*

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Influence 1** and [Leverage the Org](../cards/leverage-the-org.md).

**Type:** Culture

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 level-0 DP each; no automatic higher rank. Follow the card's equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +10 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
