# Floodwall Commons

*Neighbors took turns maintaining storm refuges along a coast where streets flooded every spring.*

*Whose door did you knock on during your first evacuation?*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - expanded culture, untested.

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Survival 1** and [Make Camp](../cards/make-camp.md).

**Type:** Culture

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 creation-stage DP each. Follow the card's equipment requirements.

**Starting coin contribution:** +5 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
