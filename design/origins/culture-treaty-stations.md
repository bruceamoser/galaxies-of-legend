*Neutral docks depended on households who knew the customs of every delegation and could settle disputes over shared facilities.*

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Influence 1** and [Read Intent](../cards/read-intent.md).

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 creation-stage DP each. Follow the card's equipment requirements.

**Starting coin contribution:** +8 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
