# Fixer

*You learned that a favour is a currency with better interest than credit.*

*Which favour is still outstanding, and who holds it?*

## Rules

**Type:** Background. Choose the paired option:

- **Subterfuge 1** and [Favour Owed](../cards/favor-owed.md).

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants provide 1 level-0 DP each; never promote a rank automatically. No discipline purchases at level 0. Follow the card's explicit equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +7 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
