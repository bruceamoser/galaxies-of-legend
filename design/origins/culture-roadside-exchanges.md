# Roadside Exchanges

*Seasonal haulers met at temporary markets beyond the settled routes. Families traveled with the fairs and shared news between ports.*

*What did you carry between fairs that someone is still waiting for?*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - expanded culture, untested.

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Trade 1** and [Find a Market](../cards/find-a-market.md).

**Type:** Culture

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 creation-stage DP each. Follow the card's equipment requirements.

**Starting coin contribution:** +8 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
