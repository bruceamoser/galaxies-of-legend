# Frontier Settlement

*The settlement survived because everyone learned to fix the thing that was about to fail.*

*What broke on the day you left, and who stayed to fix it?*

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Engineering 1** and [Keep It Running](../cards/keep-it-running.md).

**Type:** Culture

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 level-0 DP each; no automatic higher rank. Follow the card's equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +5 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
