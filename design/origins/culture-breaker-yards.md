# Breaker Yards

*Retired ships provided the metal, machinery, and living quarters for your neighborhood. Salvage crews voted on what to keep intact.*

*Which vessel did your crew save from the cutters?*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - expanded culture, untested.

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Engineering 1** and [Field Repair](../cards/field-repair.md).

**Type:** Culture

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 creation-stage DP each. Follow the card's equipment requirements.

**Starting coin contribution:** +6 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
