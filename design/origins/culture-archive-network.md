# Archive Network

*You grew up among records that had survived their original owners.*

*Which record seems to describe your future?*

**Status:** Playtest proposal - expanded culture, untested.

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Investigation 1** and [Follow Evidence](../cards/follow-evidence.md).

**Type:** Culture

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 creation-stage DP each; no automatic higher rank. Follow the card's equipment requirements; this origin grants no additional equipment or innate ancestry benefit.

**Starting coin contribution:** +7 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
