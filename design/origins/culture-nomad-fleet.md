# Nomad Fleet

*Home was a formation of ships that moved when the work did.*

*Which ship did you leave, and what did its captain say?*

## Rules

Choose the paired option:

- **Piloting 1** and [Read the Drift](../cards/read-the-drift.md).

**Type:** Culture

The discipline rank and the card are one grant. Duplicate grants give 1 level-0 DP each; no automatic higher rank. Follow the card's equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +7 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
