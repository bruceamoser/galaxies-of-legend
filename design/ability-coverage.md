# Ability coverage plan

**Status: playtest design plan.** The roster has eighteen disciplines, matching the number of fields in the current HOL catalog. Ability totals are intentionally incomplete. A field name alone does not give characters useful differentiation; the later waves must provide choices and distinct actions within each field.

## Coverage minimums

For each discipline, target at least **6 Novice, 4 Adept and 2 Master shared abilities** before treating its catalog as broadly supported. At Novice cover a reliable active task, support, a reaction or defence, a non-combat application, an alternative approach and a distinct specialist option. At higher tiers deepen the field rather than copying another discipline with larger numbers.

Class signatures are separate: each of ten classes currently has one proposed signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. A later choice wave should supply at least two alternatives at each milestone per class. The class chassis already says choose one; a single current option is not yet meaningful selection.

## Current shared-card coverage

Universal actions and class signatures are excluded. A cross-discipline prerequisite counts in each required field but does not replace a field-specific option.

## Planned waves

1. **Starter bridge, this revision:** one Novice card for each of nine new fields; twelve signatures for Operator, Outlaw, Con Artist and Technician. This supports origin grant pairs and initial class identity without pretending to complete the catalog.
2. **Novice choice:** fill every field toward six shared cards; prioritise differentiated actions and competing build choices. Review class/culture/background grants for duplicate funnels.
3. **Adept specialisation:** four cards per field, including pressured team tasks and reactions; supply second level-3 signature options.
4. **Master identity:** two cards per field, explicit narrative reach and resource limits; supply second level-7 signature options and test the full level-1 to level-10 spend.

A new ancestry will require a separate trait and equipment compatibility review. Ship abilities also require explicit personal versus ship scale and a named module. Counts are coverage gates, not balance certification.

The HOL restoration adds six treatment capabilities, universal Stabilize and medical-dose use, and the once-only One More Dawn Grit grant. These restore the foundation rather than expanding an optional ability wave.
