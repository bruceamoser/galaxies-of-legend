# Shared discipline ability coverage

Every discipline rank now has at least two matching-tier options with that rank as its sole discipline prerequisite. The audit enforces this floor. The counts below exclude class signatures, Basic Actions, cards without a rank requirement, and cards with combined requirements.

The catalog contains 206 cards outside class signatures, including Basic Actions, within OpenBaD’s 180–220 first-pass target. The expanded cards are untested proposals. The usability pass adds six free actions, six unrestricted 1 DP cards and two armour training abilities. The first wave establishes coverage, and the second adds alternate timing and approaches. Many new cards cover field procedures, setup, or emergency responses. Evaluate their usefulness and DP value in play before treating this as the final catalog.
