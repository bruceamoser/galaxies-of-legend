# Three-book Starter Kit organisation and base SA material

**Requested by Bruce:** separate starships into their own book, clean up the Player's Book to reference it, establish the base SA book and prepare the first Starter Kit with all three volumes.

## Ownership changes

- Player's Book retains the entire character catalog, all 78 character abilities and the complete inherited damage/recovery foundation including all 280 wound rows. Chapter 10 becomes a short companion-book guide. The two ship sheets are removed.
- The canonical ship manuscript moves to `design/starship-manual/manuscript/02-rules.md`, with its mechanics and numerical values preserved. The new Starship Manual adds introduction, navigation, ability pointers and quick-reference material; ship records move with it. Character signatures stay in Player's Book Chapter 4 and shared abilities in Chapter 8.
- The Sector Architect's Book adds base running/preparation guidance, eight explicitly proposed personal-scale profiles, hazard fixtures and four worksheets. It references the existing character and ship rule owners rather than copying or shortening them.

## Proposed additions and their impact

| Addition | Reason | Rules / balance impact |
|---|---|---|
| Eight ordinary NPC/device profiles | Provide concrete base-book opposition and support examples | New HP, resistance, attack and control fixtures; untested; no calibrated CV or approved encounter roster |
| Hazard authoring examples | Make triggers, intervals and responses explicit | Small damage and condition fixtures are proposals; decompression and radiation remain bounded by incomplete environmental design |
| Sector, faction, scene and campaign tools | Give the SA a repeatable preparation procedure | Narrative tools; no free mechanical grants or hidden extra action pool |
| Encounter calibration guidance | Preserve HOL's distinction between authoring seeds and tested budgets | No GOL B(L) or threat CV is assigned; HOL numbers are not silently imported as calibrated |
| Multi-book publisher and local review bundle | Make the first coordinated release reproducible | No change to character attributes, DP, Grit, wounds, healing, ship allocation or FTL numbers |

The old abbreviated attack paragraph in the Player's Book is corrected to the existing HOL single-roll/inverted-defence procedure; it does not introduce a new opposed target number. The full personal harm rules are preserved.

Release identity: Starter Kit v0.1.0, **design-review** status, three volumes. Ancestries remain placeholders. New ship/SA values and broader character content are proposals. No new starter mission, final public edition or table-readiness claim is made. The package is local and includes all three PDFs, release notes, manifest/checksums and a reproducible source archive.
