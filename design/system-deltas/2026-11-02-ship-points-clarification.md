# Ship Points and a worked ship record

Bruce clarified that ship size should grant **Ship Points (SP)**, spent when assigning dice to required and optional systems. d4 costs 1 SP and d6 costs 2 SP. The existing d8/d10/d12 prices of 3/4/5 SP and frame totals remain review proposals; this terminology correction does not approve or change their balance.

The source data, validator output, price tables, examples, companion references and ship record now use SP explicitly. The validator reports `sp_total`, `sp_spent`, `sp_remaining` and a running per-module `sp_ledger`. Data fields are `die_sp_cost` and each frame's `sp` total.

The book includes a worked 24-SP Scout Cutter ledger and a completed matching ship record, followed by the existing blank forms at the back. Required systems spend 12 SP and chosen optional systems spend 12 SP; the sheet shows 24 spent and 0 remaining. Modules, fuel, money, Hull and current wear remain separate. Upgrades during construction pay the price difference; damage does not refund SP.

No numerical pricing, capacity, quality, combat, damage, Grit, healing or FTL procedure is changed. This is a teaching and presentation correction, with explicit accounting checks.
