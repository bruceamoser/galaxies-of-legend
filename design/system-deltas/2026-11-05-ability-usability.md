# Proposed delta · Ability usability and gear training

**Status:** Untested playtest proposal.

GoL adds free basic actions and six unrestricted 1 DP Novice abilities alongside Quick Study. Free actions need no purchase. Unrestricted Novice abilities remain learned individually. Creation pools and ordinary roll resolution are unchanged.

Light and heavy armour now require their matching Armor abilities before purchase or use. Neither ability grants an item. Vac suits and shields keep their printed rules. This imports the explicit training-gate pattern from HoL. Check whether the new gates delay access to protection too much for starter crews.

The first card revision pass replaces narrow rolled tasks with explicit progress, damage, conditions, or movement. No-roll support can grant bounded attribute Boons. Later review should verify that every remaining signature and shared card meets the same design standard.
