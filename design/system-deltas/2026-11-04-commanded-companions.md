# Proposed delta · Personal companions

**Status:** Untested playtest proposal.

**Foundation:** HoL summoned forms receive a paid Main or Maneuver command, resolve on their controller's turn and receive no independent actions or Reactions. HoL uses a six-square tether, disappearance and no healing for its summoned energy forms.

**GoL adaptation:** Keep the paid packages and one-command ceiling. GoL grants one directed personal companion per controller. Drones and hires use an eight-square communication limit. Sprites require access to a working host. Loss of control interrupts activity. Physical companions remain in the scene and follow their printed repair or NPC defeat rules. Hires can refuse or end a contract.

**Reason:** OpenBaD identifies drones, sprites and hired crew as GoL's analogue to HoL's summoned forms. Equipment ownership, software permissions and a person's consent are different sources of access. The command cost keeps each from granting another independent turn.

**Balance review:** Compare command cost to direct action, especially a drone's attack and Maneuver package, sprite access to remote services and hires' repeatable support. Track blocked links, controller incapacitation, workshop costs and whether a companion displaces another explorer's specialty. Prices and level gates are test fixtures. No change to shared HP healing, wounds, Grit or starship scale.
