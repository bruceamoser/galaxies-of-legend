# Working Ten ancestry roster

**Status:** Playtest proposal adopted for the candidate manuscript.

**Previous rule:** The ancestry slot contained four placeholders. Human provided arithmetic examples only.

**Replacement:** Choose one of ten ancestries and two of its five traits. All use HP +2, base Speed 6 and +0 coins. Each chosen trait can grant one situational Boon once per scene, with at most one ancestry Boon per check. Homeworld is a story choice.

**Reason:** Exercise the full Legend origin chassis with the selected Working Ten and give each lineage a place in the setting.

**Balance impact:** Fixed contributions match the former Human arithmetic baseline. Trait frequency and strongest pairs require table review, particularly when adventures favor one environment. Appendix A species descriptions carry no additional mechanical benefits.
