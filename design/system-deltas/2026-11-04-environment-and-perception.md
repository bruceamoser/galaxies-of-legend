# Proposed delta · Environment, cover and contacts

**Status:** Untested playtest proposal.

**Foundation:** HoL's session resolution uses player Reason for active search, player Agility to hide or remain Hidden, and one roll for an incident. Its universal Hidden, condition timing, resistance, Stability, Dying and wounds remain the foundation.

**GoL additions:** Personal sight and obscurity bands, directional cover DR, bounded sensor contacts, bracing and zero-gravity drift, air budgets, pressure and suffocation damage, thermal clocks and Radiant exposure fixtures. The Player's Book owns these procedures. The SA book owns encounter placement and clocks. The Starship Manual owns system dice, ship sensors and compartment life-support reserve.

**Departures from the OpenBaD draft:** Keep one roll for hiding and discovery. Darkness is a sight constraint. Sensor contact is evidence, not a lock. Cover DR is obstacle protection and applies once after personal resistance. Vacuum and exhausted air deal distinct damage events through ordinary HP and Dying, without a new Suffocating condition. Radiation deals Radiant damage and can cause a wound through the ordinary triggers. Thermal exposure uses existing Burning and Freezing.

**Compatibility and impact:** Shadow Step now explicitly rolls Agility. Sensor Sweep and the sensor pack reveal measurable evidence and use existing ship sensor checks when used at a station. Vac suits state their pressure protection, air supply and Radiant resistance explicitly. Test whether cover prolongs exchanges, whether the vacuum escape window allows rescue, and whether sensor limits create useful choices without duplicate rolls.
