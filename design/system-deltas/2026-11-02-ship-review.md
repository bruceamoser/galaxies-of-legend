# Ship-system review delta - 2026-11-02

**Status: proposed / playtest; awaiting Bruce's design review.** No character attributes, chassis, grants, DP prices, prerequisites or ability card texts change.

Canonical teaching rules: `../starship-manual/manuscript/02-rules.md`. The Player's Book consumes that source directly. This delta records additions and differences from the previous merged draft.

| Rule changed | Proposed replacement | Reason | Balance impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handling called ship Agility, without a use procedure | Compare Handling to grant a single Boon or Bane in contested powered manoeuvres, attacks and defence; never add it to an attribute | Preserve one-attribute core roll | Better-handling ships gain substantial offensive and defensive advantage; needs review |
| No ship weapon damage or defence outcomes | Two explicit ship-scale mounts; player attack reads damage directly; player defence inverts incoming damage band | Make example encounters readable | Defines initial lethality; does not scale personal cards automatically |
| Unclear shield load and personal minimum damage | Defences rolls after each incoming attack; offline removes printed DR; ship damage can be zero | Connect shields to their die and preserve fully absorbed hits | Frequent attacks can exhaust shields even without Hull loss |
| Vague station permissions and extra Strong system damage | Universal station actions, personal turn budgets, one shot per mount per round; only printed Strong effects apply | Give every crew member a baseline and bound action count | Crew and mounts limit output; avoids automatic Strong system degradation |
| Power transfer durations and offline targets unspecified | Basic transfer persists, cannot restart offline receiver, Weak donor goes offline at scene end; Power Routing remains its separate temporary card | Separate baseline from signature and track actual wear | Basic transfer is a severe sacrifice; temporary boosts cannot erase wear |
| Hull yard repair conflicts with general component HP repair | Field Repair HP option does not repair Hull; shipyard structural procedure; spare-parts lots gate ship repair abilities | Keep hull and system damage distinct | Removes unlimited toolkit restoration; strong abilities remain valuable but require stock |
| Temporary effects and fragile repairs ambiguous | Underlying current die plus temporary modifiers; wear affects underlying die; next 1 cancels fragile repair gain and applies normal wear | Make ending effects deterministic | Temporary bonuses do not immunize wear; fragile repairs can fail sharply |
| FTL fuel and time unspecified; arrival check ambiguous | Route units map to 3/6/9; one fuel and ten minutes per segment; recorded completing navigation band sets arrival | Make preparation and ship sheet concrete | Long jumps consume more fuel, time and load checks |
| Emergency Jump seemed to waive preparation | Strong bypasses separate commitment action only after both tracks complete; costly emergency charging fills one segment per action | Keep two-track premise while permitting urgent departures | Fuel reserve and capacitor risk become escape constraints |
| Offline life support and 0 Hull had no procedure | Explicit air reserve and disabled-ship emergency clocks | Make danger visible rather than automatic destruction | Creates rescue and damage-control time; abstract clock lengths remain provisional |

Economic prices, ship advancement, further weapons and boarding are deferred. All new timing, stock, damage, support and clock values are review proposals, not table-validated balance.
