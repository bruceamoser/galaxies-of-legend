# Roster, ancestry and ship construction delta - 2026-11-02

**Status: playtest proposal, untested.** Requested by Bruce after reviewing the first PDF. This supersedes the endurance-only parts of the earlier ship review delta. No six-attribute values, character DP pool, tier price, rank price bands or level progression change.

| Rule changed | Proposed replacement | Reason | Balance impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nine broad disciplines | Eighteen scoped fields, matching HOL's field count; new Armor, Athletics, Survival, Computing, Robotics, Trade, Investigation, Command, Xenology | Support different builds and reduce catch-all disciplines | Expanded affinity profiles alter rank affordability; new fields require later ability coverage |
| Six classes | Ten: existing six plus Operator, Outlaw, Con Artist, Technician | Create distinct covert, frontier, deception and machine-specialist identities | Twelve new untested signatures; no new class-exclusive subsystem |
| Four cultures/four backgrounds | Twelve each, with indivisible rank/card grants | More origin combinations and routes into training | Expanded grant overlap and duplicate DP opportunities require review |
| Homeworld fills biological origin slot | Ancestry placeholders await Bruce; old environmental packages are legacy only | Aliens need physiology separate from birthplace or culture | No completed ancestry builds yet; Human arithmetic scaffold is +2 HP, Speed 6, +0 coins, no traits |
| System die means only endurance | Installed die sets equipment quality, scope, capacity and wear ceiling; current condition can reduce active performance | d6 sensors must be better than d4 sensors | Boons combine with Handling within core cap; damaged systems lose capability as well as endurance |
| Unbudgeted ship seed values | Five frames with points, die ceilings, module limits and mandatory five systems | Enable a ship builder with auditable sacrifices | Three replacement example ships use exact budgets; Hull/Handling now derive from frame |
| Shields only change at offline | Effective die sets DR at each step | Make shield allocation and condition visible | Every degradation can increase later Hull damage |
| One shared weapons die | Separate paid module per mount, own current die, effective damage and operator | Charge for firepower and track damage correctly | Extra mounts require crew and positions, one shot each per round |
| Navigation always gets a computer Boon | One primary equipment grade replaces the automatic Boon | Avoid stacking computer quality twice | d4 imposes a Bane, d6 neutral, d8/d10/d12 grant 1/2/3 Boons |
| Fixed 3/6/9 FTL and ten-minute charge rate | Drive range 1-5; three segments/progress per route unit; capacitor storage 3-15 and rate 10/8/6/4/2 minutes | Higher-grade drive/bank buys actual capability | Wear may invalidate a planned route or vent excess charge; fuel is not refunded |
| Transfers can appear to create better hardware | Current-die transfer cannot exceed installed capability; may add endurance | Prevent construction budget bypass | Full service restores purchased hardware, not an unbudgeted upgrade |

Readability correction: ability cards use larger text, more leading, separated outcomes, high-contrast marks and native whole-card flow. Class-cost tables derive column count from actual headers and split ten classes into two five-class groups; schema checks reject misaligned rows.

The source includes ship allocation validation and rejection tests. These establish structural consistency, not game balance. Prices, oversized weapons, finished ancestries and broad ability waves remain open.
