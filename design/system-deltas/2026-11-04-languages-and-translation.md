# Proposed delta · Languages and translation

**Status:** Untested playtest proposal.

**Foundation:** HoL grants the shared trade language and one ordinary language at creation, separates literacy restrictions and requires a usable communication channel.

**GoL adaptation:** Use Galactic Standard, add ten named language traditions associated with the Working Ten systems, and permit any explorer to choose any ordinary language. Add bounded translator libraries, and use Reason for uncertain first-contact signaling. Decryption uses technical checks.

**Reason:** Give homeworlds and ports distinctive languages that can be used in play. Cross-system communities, diaspora and individual upbringing remain part of character creation.

**Balance:** The free starting choices grant no DP, ancestry trait, discipline bonus, extra sense or clearance. Unlike HoL's specific Another Tongue purchase, this draft follows OpenBaD and uses explicit campaign grants for later fluency. The 60-coin translator and its included library are provisional.
