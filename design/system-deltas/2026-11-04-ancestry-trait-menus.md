# Working Ten trait menus

**Status:** Playtest proposal.

**Previous rule.** Each ancestry supplied five narrow checks with the same once-per-scene Boon. Players chose two.

**Replacement.** Players still choose two of five. Each trait now specifies its own effect and limits. The menus use passive resistances, Stability, movement costs, reach, Reactions, Maneuvers, progress and a small number of broader Boons. Starting HP, Speed and coin values remain the common chassis before selected traits are applied. Character path suggestions describe upbringing and work.

**Reason.** Give ancestry a recognizable effect in play and more than one viable build direction. The HoL trait catalog informed the mix of permissions, passives and triggered effects. Paizo’s Starfinder ancestry writeups and Mongoose’s Traveller species profiles informed the separation of physiology, society and character hooks.

**Balance impact.** Compare pairs rather than single traits. Priorities are repeatable movement permissions, passive Physical resistance, forced movement reactions, the Human DP grants and the Pellori treatment progress. Extra movement and actions use the printed timing. Resistance uses the shared highest-source rule. Treatment uses the existing capability and attempt limit.
