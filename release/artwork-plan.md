# Artwork production plan — initial playtest and book expansion

## Direction

Show people doing science, hazardous work, negotiation, exploration and survival on planets and stations. Ship scenes support that range. Follow the Brand Guide: Void Black #0A0A0C, Stellar White #F4F4F6, Nebula Teal #0E7A85, Warning Crimson #D92027, Mythic Gold #D4AF37. Oswald headings, Roboto body/data, Cinzel only for chapter titles, artifacts and LEGEND. Painterly science-fiction adventure, readable silhouettes, worn practical equipment, deep-space teal and gold accents. Preserve the existing cover and separate crest. No text baked into artwork. Homeworld is an upbringing, not a biological species or uniform appearance.

## Additional explanatory art

These require explicit new layout placements: one annotated ship-system diagram, one range-band diagram and one FTL preparation diagram. Build diagrams from editable vector sources so rule changes remain cheap. Add three sample-ship silhouettes (Scout Cutter, Free Trader, System Shuttle), one equipment plate with the ten catalog items, and a character-creation example only after the rules and examples stabilize. Do not commission 48 ability illustrations for this release; the cards must remain compact and legible.

## Future books

Only the Player’s Book has a manuscript/build in this repository. Reserve a future Sector Architect book for adversary portraits, encounter maps, settlement scenes, hazards and scenario handouts. Derive its exact asset list from an approved contents list and actual encounters. Do not invent a bestiary or commission species illustrations before their rules exist. Reuse equipment and ship silhouettes across books and sheets.

## Delivery and review

1. Approve one Scientist portrait and one Beltborn environment as style anchors.
2. Produce the other four homeworlds/classes batches as individual files, reviewing silhouette, equipment consistency and planet/station representation.
3. Complete cultures/backgrounds and explanatory diagrams after layout review.
4. Keep a manifest with ID, brief, filename, source/artist, creation method, usage rights, approval status, dimensions and revision. Record AI generation where applicable; do not assume clearance from file presence.
5. Supply layered masters when available, sRGB PNG exports, no lettering or watermark. Transparent background for standalone figures/equipment; full backgrounds for environments. Minimum 300 ppi at final placement, including any crop; retain larger masters. Confirm bleed and printer profile only when a printer is chosen.
6. Build the PDF and inspect each placement for crop, face/hand/tool errors, contrast and space occupied by rules. Add meaningful alternative descriptions with layout integration.

## Acceptance

No image grants a mechanical permission. Depicted gear must match the catalog. Represent multiple ages, builds and backgrounds across the cast. Crew stations must never be visually assigned as exclusive class roles. Mark placeholders as production work, and keep the initial release labeled playtest.
