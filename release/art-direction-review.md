## Working scout cutter

Asset: `design/player-book/art/interior/ship-review-cutter.png`.

### Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-width chapter opening illustration for Galaxies of Legend, a science-fiction tabletop RPG about a small crew and a lived-in frontier ship. Create a cinematic landscape illustration, wide 3:2 composition, an original compact scout cutter hanging just outside an industrial orbital repair dock over the curved blue-grey limb of a remote planet. Main subject fills the center: believable asymmetrical small working starship, angular off-white and charcoal hull plates with restrained blue-teal panels and small warning-red maintenance markings, visible patched plating, recessed manoeuvring thrusters, cockpit, radiator and compact weapon mount. A tiny suited engineer on a tether establishes scale. Rich painterly science-fiction book illustration with crisp material details and atmospheric depth, broad dark negative space around the silhouette, cool teal docking lights and small crimson hazard lamps. No gold ornament, no fantasy elements, no UI, no panels, no borders, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. This is an evocative illustration, not a labeled schematic.

## Frontier crew

Asset: `design/player-book/art/interior/frontier-crew-review.png`.

### Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide full-width chapter-opening art for Galaxies of Legend, science-fiction tabletop RPG. Cinematic lived-in science fiction with a small dose of bold pulp adventure. Five distinct frontier crewmates on a rough landing platform beside their weathered compact scout ship at a remote settlement: an engineer with a tool roll, a survey explorer holding a sensor, a medic with a field bag, an envoy in practical travel clothes, and an armored guard with a lowered weapon. Adult people with varied faces, builds and skin tones, practical layered spacewear, believable worn equipment. Wide 3:2 landscape composition; characters in lower middle foreground, immense blue-grey alien escarpment, small settlement lights and orbiting moon in atmospheric background. Painterly book illustration, dramatic sense of scale and discovery, charcoal, steel blue, blue-teal with small warning-red fabric accents, bright readable natural faces. No fantasy robes, no gold trim, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no borders, no watermark.
