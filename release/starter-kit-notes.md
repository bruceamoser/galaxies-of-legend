# Galaxies of Legend v0.1.1 · Expanded Starter Kit review

This release publishes refreshed PDFs for all three books and the matching source, ship records, and worked Scout Cutter example.

- **Player’s Book.** Ten illustrated ancestries and their star systems, expanded origins and equipment, 90 class signatures with three choices per milestone, and 206 other cards including Basic Actions. The book distinguishes Basic Actions from learned Universal Abilities and adds discipline glyphs, action badges, and rule links. The full 280-result wound reference remains in Chapter 7.
- **Starship Manual.** Updated reading guide, construction and operation explanations, and companion references. The worked 24-SP Scout Cutter and fillable painted ship sheets are included.
- **Sector Architect’s Book.** Expanded preparation, language and environmental guidance, 67 adversaries across six families, 81 reusable adversary abilities, 27 companion profiles, and six worked encounters with provisional Combat Value estimates.

This is a design-review prerelease. The new rules and catalog entries are untested, and the CV values are uncalibrated. The package checks verify build and content integrity.
