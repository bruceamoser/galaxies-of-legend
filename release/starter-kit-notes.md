# Starter Kit v0.1.0 - three-book review notes

This edition separates starship material from the Player's Book and adds the base Sector Architect's Book.

## Changes

The Player's Book retains the full restored HOL foundation: damage/resistance/weakness, limited Grit, Dying, stabilization, marks, Last Words, the complete conditions, six treatment capabilities and all five 56-entry wound tables. No compact replacement table is used.

The Starship Manual owns the moved construction/operating manuscript and ship sheets. Its frame budgets, die costs, capability tables, ship damage, repair and FTL procedures are unchanged by the book split. The manual adds a reading guide, full-card pointers and a quick reference.

The base SA book establishes adjudication and preparation procedures, makes opposition and hazard examples explicit, and supplies reusable sector/scene/encounter/review records. Its eight threat profiles and numerical hazards remain untested proposals with no calibrated GOL CV.

## Pending design work

Bruce's ancestry material; broader ability waves; final interior illustrations; ship acquisition and economic calibration; full environmental procedures; encounter calibration; a complete bestiary and the first starter mission. These remain open and are not concealed by the three-book packaging.

## Painted ship records

This revision adopts the approved painted raster direction for ship records. Five hulls provide 8/12/16/22/30 inset system panels matching existing module limits; the sixth page completes the 24-SP Scout Cutter. The interactive records are integrated into the manual and provided separately. Their 394 fields have matching logical values and widget appearances. SP arithmetic remains manual. Hull placement and depicted thrusters add no mechanics or free equipment. Heavy and Capital use larger 17-by-11-inch sheets to retain room for their additional panels.

## Validation scope

Catalog/link checks, all existing HOL/ship regression tests, book-boundary checks, independent Typst compilation, all 280 wound-code presence checks, profile/sheet inclusion checks, rendered page review, archive integrity and SHA-256 verification. These checks establish content/packaging consistency, not game balance.

## Ship Points clarification and worked sheet

Ship size supplies Ship Points (SP), and every installed module die spends them. The existing prices are now explicit SP: d4 1, d6 2, d8 3, d10 4 and d12 5. The higher-die prices and frame totals remain proposals. No construction price or performance number changed. A running 24-SP Scout Cutter ledger and completed matching sheet now accompany the blank construction form. The CLI reports total, spent and remaining SP with a per-module ledger.
